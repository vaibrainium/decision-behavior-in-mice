# Criteria commitment: outline round 1

Sources used: `np/spine.md`, `np/config.md`, `np/ledger/claims.csv`, `np/lit/gaps.md`, `review-rubrics.md`, and `section-specs.md` (no outline or section brief seen).

| ID | Criterion | What I will look for | BLOCK if | REVISE if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Contract and locked-spine fidelity | The outline preserves the locked thesis, OCAR story, glossary distinctions, hypotheses, and stated non-claims; it treats the tested object as a model-and-input pipeline and the model comparison as relative to the tested implementations. | It contradicts the thesis, claims unique accumulation or a uniquely sensory/decision/response mechanism, presents primacy/recency as established, or promotes the unreviewed `tau_p` pilot. | Emphasis, ordering, terminology, title language, or hedging drifts from the locked spine without directly contradicting it. |
| K2 | Every approved claim has one primary placement | Each approved claim C001–C021 is assigned to exactly one primary section, Results subsection, figure, or supplementary result, with a traceable claim ID; necessary Abstract or Discussion restatements are labelled as synthesis/delivery rather than duplicate primary analyses. | Any approved claim is absent, an unapproved scientific claim is introduced as settled, or incompatible sections are assigned the same primary evidential work. | A claim has duplicate or ambiguous primary placement, or its assigned location does not match its ledger type and evidence. |
| K3 | One central claim per figure | Figures 1–5 and Supplementary Figure S6 each have one explicit central finding that matches the spine; C009 is visibly subordinate to Figure 5's pipeline-mismatch claim rather than a competing headline. | A figure's central claim contradicts the spine, lacks an approved claim, or combines unrelated conclusions that require different evidence. | A figure carries multiple co-equal messages, or panels and supplementary placement do not form a coherent test of its single central claim. |
| K4 | Claim-first Results architecture | Results subsections are in figure order and use full, evidence-calibrated claims as headings: task/constraints → reconstructed input → held-out control comparison → randomized behavioural pulse effect → failed pulse generalization, with S6 negative outcomes explicitly signposted. | The sequence hides or reverses the paper's decisive comparison, or a heading states a mechanistic/causal conclusion the evidence cannot support. | Headings name topics or procedures instead of findings, do not read as an extended abstract, or do not map one-to-one onto the figure sequence. |
| K5 | Results and Methods mirror each other | Every Results analysis, model comparison, calibration, stimulus audit, exploratory diagnostic, and supplementary outcome has a corresponding Methods subsection in matching order where feasible; Methods also covers subjects/ethics, randomization, units of analysis, fitting/recovery, censoring, statistics, and data/code availability. | A reported result has no planned method, or a method essential to interpreting a central result is omitted. | Mirroring/order is unclear, reporting-checklist items are not assigned, or method ownership of a figure/supplement is ambiguous. |
| K6 | OCAR and Challenge–Resolution close cleanly | The Introduction narrows from model identifiability to the exact control-to-pulse generalization Challenge; Results perform the stated Action; Discussion D1 answers that Challenge with the locked Resolution, and H1/H2 are resolved only by the relative Figure 3 comparison while H3 is addressed by Figures 4–5 and S6. | The Resolution answers a different question, uses the pulse strategy test to decide H1/H2 despite inadequate sensitivity, or converts a pipeline failure into proof against accumulation. | The Challenge arrives late or vaguely, hypotheses lack distinct predictions/deciding figures, or D1 requires readers to reconstruct the answer themselves. |
| K7 | Promises are seeded and discharged | Every Introduction promise is entered with a named delivery location; the control-model comparison is delivered in Figure 3, pulse generalization in Figures 4–5, gate/sensitivity disclosures in Results/S6 and Discussion, and the Discussion separates behavioural effect from mechanism. | A central promise has no delivery location or the outline promises a claim the approved evidence cannot deliver. | Promise ownership is ambiguous, a promised control/limitation is deferred without a destination, or sections create orphan promises. |
| K8 | Mandatory failed-gate and low-sensitivity record remains visible | The outline requires the original and gain-revised predictive-gate failures, inadequate pulse-strategy sensitivity, withheld confirmatory timing test, and the distinction between low sensitivity and evidence of absence in Results/S6 and Discussion—not only in Methods. | Any of these outcomes is omitted, recast as evidence for equivalence/no primacy, or replaced by an unvalidated timing/mechanism claim. | The material is technically present but buried, fragmented, euphemized, or lacks a clear explanation of why the confirmatory timing test was not run. |
| K9 | Mouse novelty is bounded and cross-species evidence is respected | The Introduction states that mouse continuous-RDK behaviour, discrete visual accumulation tasks, and visual choice/RT diffusion modelling already exist; novelty is limited to a bounded search finding no prior mouse study with the full continuous-RDK + randomized signed-pulse + frozen control-only joint-transfer combination, with strong macaque evidence presented as a comparator. | The outline says or implies “first,” “nobody has shown,” first mouse dynamic-visual accumulation/pulse/DDM/RDK work, or treats the present behavioural mismatch as refuting macaque neural accumulation evidence. | The bounded-search qualifier, exact combination, closest mouse precedents, or macaque reconciliation is missing or too weak. |
| K10 | Evidence scope, negative results, and statistical units are planned honestly | Own-result placements preserve ledger limits: group descriptive temporal-profile comparison without an invented paired test; C009 limited to PSUIM6/7; emitted-response timing not neural integration time; subject-level group inference; known replay, model-fit, and identifiability limitations assigned to Results/Discussion. | The outline upgrades descriptive/exploratory evidence to confirmatory inference, generalizes a two-mouse diagnostic, invents statistical support, or conditions away no-response/pre-onset outcomes in the stated key control. | Evidence labels, units of analysis, necessary caveats, or locations for null/negative results are incomplete. |
| K11 | eLife main-text and front-matter budgets are credible | Section budgets for Introduction + Results + Discussion total no more than the suggested 5,000-word main-text budget, with Methods, references, and legends budgeted separately; Abstract is 150–200 words, title is planned at no more than 120 characters, and the 15–30-word third-person impact statement plus required front matter have destinations. | The proposed structure cannot accommodate the central evidence and mandatory negative result within a coherent eLife article, or omits required scientific sections. | Budgets do not sum, exceed the stated targets without justification, or required front matter/expanded thesis material is not assigned. |
| K12 | Missing facts remain explicit and briefs are draftable | Unknown animal details, ethics identifiers, sample-size rationale, blinding, author order/roles/affiliations, funding, competing interests, data/code availability, AI disclosure wording, and any other unsupported detail remain `[MISSING: ...]`; every section brief has the required purpose, reader question, moves, claim/number/source/figure mappings, promises, risks, and acceptance criteria. | Any missing fact, method, number, source, authorship item, or compliance statement is guessed or fabricated. | A missing item lacks a specific placeholder/owner, or a brief is too incomplete to draft independently and traceably. |
