# Statistics criteria commitment: methods round 1

Sources used: `np/spine.md`, `np/config.md`, `np/briefs/methods.md`, `review-rubrics.md`, and the `np-stats-reviewer` role guide (no file under `np/drafts/` seen).

| ID | Criterion | What I will look for | P0 — must fix | P1 — should fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Estimands, populations and nesting are declared before tests | Each analysis names its target population, estimand and analyzed set. Mice are the independent unit for group inference; sessions, scheduled cells and trials remain nested within mouse and are used only for within-mouse estimation or explicitly descriptive summaries. The construction and weighting of one mouse-level estimate per group contrast are reproducible. | A group claim treats trials, cells or sessions as independent replicates, or the dependence structure/weighting can change the answer without disclosure. | An `n` lacks its unit, the mouse-level aggregation is underspecified, or descriptive lower-level summaries are not clearly separated from inference. |
| S2 | Assignment and the pulse estimand preserve the randomized comparison | The assignment audit establishes that signed pulse category was assigned before outcome within coherence-defined schedules. The primary contrast is defined within supported mouse × session × signed-coherence × onset cells, with support rules and equal-mouse group weighting; scheduled and historical labels are distinguished. | Outcome-conditioned pulse classification/inclusion, unsupported pooling, or post-outcome assignment could manufacture the directional effect. | The randomization unit, common-support rule, cell weights, assignment audit, or distinction between scheduled assignment and delivered exposure is incomplete. |
| S3 | Pre-onset responses, no responses and censoring are handled without conditioning on outcome | Pre-onset responses and `no response by 5 s` remain valid assigned outcomes in the pulse estimand. The 5-s analysis cutoff is distinguished from task termination. Exclusions—including invalid-duration or legacy-input omissions—are enumerated with reasons and assessed for outcome dependence; control pseudo-onsets/shams follow an explicit rule. | The primary effect excludes pre-onset/no-response outcomes, conflates censoring with missingness, or silently drops outcome-dependent rows. | Counts/reasons for exclusions, analysis-cutoff semantics, pseudo-onset construction, or a sensitivity to known label/input discrepancies are not reproducible. |
| S4 | Choice, response probability and RT enter the model through a complete likelihood/objective | The model equations and observation mapping specify choice/response probabilities, the no-response mass, conditional RT-bin construction, nondecision time, fast-response mixture, smoothing and the rule for including RT objective terms. Censoring and empty/sparse RT cells are handled explicitly; a quantile objective is not called a calibrated likelihood if it is not one. | Omitted censoring or response-probability terms materially redefine the fitted model, or means replace the promised RT distributions. | Bin edges, sparse-cell handling, smoothing, mixture/lapse/bias parameterization, or likelihood-versus-objective terminology is unclear. |
| S5 | Control-model selection is genuinely held out and its threshold has the correct meaning | Five-fold control-only cross-validation specifies the split unit, fold construction, parameter refitting, score direction, aggregation across folds/mice, simulation repetitions and Monte Carlo working-threshold construction. Held-out selection is kept separate from in-sample fit-quality displays, and score components are not treated as independently selected wins. | Test information leaks into fitting/selection, the working threshold is presented as a population p value, or the comparison is generalized beyond the tested implementations. | Split stratification, fold dependence, Monte Carlo error, score aggregation, or the selection-versus-adequacy distinction is underspecified. |
| S6 | Recovery and identifiability qualify model interpretation | Model/parameter recovery states the generators, fitted candidates, repetitions, decision rule and false-positive/confusion summaries. Boundary behavior, weakly recovered parameters, high-coherence error/late-response misfit and guess-delay limitations are linked to the claims they constrain; generic recovery citations do not substitute for project-specific checks. | Unrecovered or boundary parameters support a mechanistic claim, or selection recovery is omitted for the reported comparison. | Generator coverage, recovery metric, Monte Carlo uncertainty, parameter bounds, or the consequences of known failures are incomplete. |
| S7 | Pulse prediction and gain diagnostics keep selection independent from evaluation | Frozen control parameters, prediction inputs/files, simulation seeds/repetitions and common-row restrictions are stated. Mouse-specific pulse-gain cross-validation defines training/test partitions and candidate grid; choice-optimal gain selection is evaluated on held-out choice and a separately defined remaining objective without reusing the selection data. Post-gate gain profiles are labelled exploratory. | Recorded pulse outcomes enter a supposedly frozen control fit, or the same data/objective are used for selection and claimed held-out validation. | Split construction, common-row intersection, objective decomposition, RT-term rule, weak gain identification, or Monte Carlo precision is unclear. |
| S8 | The model–data timing comparison is explicitly descriptive | Observed and predicted pulse-aligned curves/window increments use identical eligible rows, outcomes, time origin, endpoint and mouse weights. No paired inferential test is implied unless one is actually specified and approved; cumulative carry-forward and pointwise intervals are defined. | A visual/descriptive comparison is assigned an invented significance test or interpreted as temporal weighting, neural latency or processing-stage localization. | Common-row/weight alignment, uncertainty construction, endpoint/window choice, or the descriptive status is not explicit. |
| S9 | The two-mouse C009 diagnostic cannot become group inference | The eligibility/selection rule for PSUIM6 and PSUIM7 is stated without calling them representative. Per-mouse gain profiles and held-out changes are the inferential scope; other mice's weak identification is shown or summarized, and no cohort test is constructed from the selected pair. | The two mice are generalized to the ten-mouse population or chosen post hoc for an unqualified confirmatory claim. | The selection rationale, per-mouse replication, uncertainty/Monte Carlo precision, or reason other mice are non-informative is missing. |
| S10 | Multiplicity, uncertainty, assumptions and stopping are auditable | Every inferential family is named. The directional-effect family uses the stated Holm adjustment; unadjusted and adjusted p values are distinguishable; time-course bands are pointwise, not simultaneous. Mouse-level effect estimates and intervals lead reporting, the suitability/robustness of t intervals at ten mice is addressed, and original/revised gates plus the rule withholding the timing test are fully specified. | Multiple testing is left uncorrected for a central claim, optional stopping is hidden, or the unrun timing test is described as performed. | Tail choice, family membership, effect-size/interval definition, robustness/estimator simulation, amendment chronology, or low-sensitivity interpretation is incomplete. |
| S11 | Reproducibility is figure-level and availability claims are real | For every Results figure/S6 analysis, Methods identify versioned inputs, analysis script/command, software environment, random seeds, output path, and code/commit sufficient to regenerate it without modifying raw data. Data/code access, sample-size rationale and blinding remain documented facts or exact `[MISSING: ...]` placeholders. | A method, version, repository, availability condition, sample-size decision or blinding statement is invented, or a central figure cannot in principle be regenerated. | Paths/commands/seeds/versions are too vague, simulation determinism and output provenance are missing, or unresolved availability facts are silently omitted. |
