# Criteria commitment: figure_legends round 1

Sources used: `np/spine.md`, `np/config.md`, `np/briefs/figure_legends.md`, `review-rubrics.md`, and `section-specs.md` (no draft seen).

| ID | Criterion | What I will look for | BLOCK if | REVISE if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Locked scope with no new primary claims | Legends restate C002–C007/C009 only as figure cross-references, preserve glossary terms and pipeline-level limits, and add no interpretation, novelty or mechanism claim. | A legend invents a result, contradicts the spine, asserts primacy/unique accumulation/localization, or creates a new primary claim. | Wording or hedging drifts from the Results claim without a direct contradiction. |
| K2 | Order, budget and finding-as-title | Legends appear Figure 1–5 then S6, total no more than 1,650 words, and each opens with a stand-alone finding sentence matching its Results heading; Figure 5's title states C006 alone. | A figure lacks a finding title or its title asserts an unsupported conclusion. | Order/budget is wrong, titles are topical, or C009 shares the Figure 5 headline. |
| K3 | Complete panel-by-panel description | Every visible panel letter is described in order, including inputs, plotted variables, scales/units, line/marker conventions, individual-versus-group displays and model settings; no nonexistent panel is described. | A central panel cannot be interpreted from the legend or the legend materially misdescribes what is plotted. | A panel, axis quantity, line style, marker, normalization, or panel linkage is undefined. |
| K4 | Denominators and units stand alone | Every panel states relevant `n` and what it counts—mice, mouse-sessions, trials, supported cells, seeds/stimuli, fit files or simulated datasets—and separates descriptive lower-level units from mouse-level inference. | A denominator is wrong/invented or pseudoreplication is implied. | A panel uses generic `n`, omits changing denominators, or confuses stimulus/seed and animal units. |
| K5 | Uncertainty, tests and multiplicity are defined | Every band/error bar names its statistic and level; tests, correction families, asterisks and tailing are defined; Figure 4 bands are explicitly pointwise, not simultaneous; descriptive comparisons are not assigned p values. | An inferential symbol/test is unexplained or a significance claim is created from overlap/non-overlap alone. | CI/SEM/SD type, level, correction, test unit, or pointwise status is missing or inconsistent. |
| K6 | Figure 1 terminology and outcome constraints | The legend defines task/schedule panels, rig schedules, psychometric/RT/no-response displays, individual/group conventions, denominators, and the exact `no response by 5 s` analysis-cutoff terminology without calling it timeout/task deadline. | Outcome categories or timing rules are materially wrong. | Cutoff, schedules, plotting conventions, or denominator definitions are incomplete. |
| K7 | Figure 2 reconstruction caveats and units | The replay/filter/calibration panels define stimulus/seed versus mouse units, motion-energy operations and alternatives while stating replay uncertainty and avoiding frame-perfect-display or neural-latency language. | The legend presents replay as measured display truth or filter delay as biological latency. | Calibration, units, replay alternatives, filter conventions, or uncertainty caveats are incomplete. |
| K8 | Figure 3 comparison conventions | The legend defines both model implementations, held-out versus in-sample panels, score sign, working-threshold caveat, component-score status and recovery pointer without calling the threshold a p value or interpreting components independently. | It changes the comparison or overstates relative support as unique model proof. | Score sign, threshold origin, split status, parameter/recovery caveat, or plotted model convention is unclear. |
| K9 | Figure 4 estimand and cumulative-curve scope | The legend defines the directional contrast, common-support weights, pulse/control support distinction, right/left/no-response components, endpoint, animal/trial/cell `n`, Student-t intervals, Holm family, cumulative carry-forward and pointwise bands. | Outcome-conditioned inclusion or a wrong estimand is described, or the curve is called an integration kernel/primacy result. | Support differences, retained pre-onset/no-response trials, uncertainty, or response-associated timing scope is missing. |
| K10 | Figure 5 hierarchy and diagnostic restrictions | C006 alone titles Figure 5; common-row observed/predicted curves, gain settings, window increments and descriptive group comparison are defined first; C009 is a selected subordinate PSUIM6/PSUIM7 diagnostic—not “representative”—with objective terms and legacy-input intersection defined. | The two mice are generalized to the cohort, the overlay becomes a paired inferential claim, or mismatch is localized to a unique mechanism. | C009 hierarchy, selection rationale, gain/objective definitions, common-row restriction, or Monte Carlo limitation is unclear. |
| K11 | S6 keeps distinct failures and sensitivity questions separate | S6 defines original/revised gates, RT coverage, strategy recovery, decay/primacy calibration and panel-specific denominators; it states the timing test was not run and low sensitivity is not evidence of no primacy/equivalence. | Mandatory S6 content is omitted or an inconclusive calibration is recast as a null mechanism result. | Gate rules, calibration levels/denominators, distinct questions, or stopping statement is unclear. |
| K12 | Number-token and cross-section consistency | Every hard value is a matching `{{N###}}` token with the same meaning/unit as Results, all claim markers are cross-references, and legends use no unsupported citations or unregistered values. | A value/claim is fabricated or mismatched, or the legend contradicts Results. | Token context, terminology, or Results/legend wording differs enough to hinder audit or stand-alone reading. |
