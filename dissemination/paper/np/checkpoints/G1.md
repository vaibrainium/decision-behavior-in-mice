## Recommended thesis

> Joint held-out prediction on control trials favoured the tested accumulator over a rapid-forgetting implementation, but randomized motion pulses exposed a failure of the tested model-and-input pipelines to generalize to transient strong evidence.

This is the architect's balanced framing after challenge. It makes the control-model comparison relative, treats the pulse analysis as an out-of-condition test, and does not attribute the mismatch uniquely to sensory, decision or response dynamics.
