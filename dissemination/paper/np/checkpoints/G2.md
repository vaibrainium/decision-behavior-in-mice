## Defensible gap

Prior studies show that joint choice/response-time measurements and motion pulses can constrain decision models, including strong recent macaque neural evidence consistent with bounded accumulation. Mouse continuous-RDK psychophysics, dynamic discrete visual accumulation and visual choice/RT diffusion modelling are also established separately. The bounded search did not identify a mouse study combining continuous noisy RDK, randomized signed motion pulses and a frozen control-only model evaluated jointly against choice, response probability and response-time outcomes. This paper addresses that narrow predictive-transfer question; it does not claim the first mouse dynamic-visual task, pulse experiment, accumulation result, RDK task or joint choice/RT model.

## Closest-work boundary

- Stine et al. 2023 and Steinemann et al. 2024 used weak randomized 100-ms pulses with macaque choice/RT and neural analyses, but did not report control-only fitting followed by frozen pulse prediction.
- Parés-Pujolràs et al. 2026 v3 fit across the pulse-task conditions rather than using pulses as a held-out quantitative test.
- These studies strengthen evidence for accumulation in other preparations and narrow the paper's claim to failure of this mouse model-and-input pipeline under a strong perturbation.
- Douglas 2006, Stirman 2016, Marques 2018 and Samonds 2018 establish mouse/rodent continuous-RDK psychophysics; Pinto 2018, Odoemene 2018 and Koay 2020 establish dynamic discrete visual evidence accumulation; You 2022 and Caballero 2022 establish mouse visual DDM/RT modelling. None supplied the exact held-out signed-motion-pulse transfer test in the bounded search.

## Flags

- The broad novelty statement “nobody has shown this in mice with a dynamic visual decision-making task” is false. Use only “in a bounded search, we found no prior mouse study combining...” followed by the exact task/model conjunction; do not write “first” or “nobody.”
- Several retained notes are abstract-only and may support only the narrow statements recorded in those notes.
