# Criteria commitment: S6 panel, devil's advocate

Role: `np-devils-advocate`  
Mode: panel  
Committed before reading `manuscript.md` or manuscript drafts.  
Sources used for commitment: neuropaper S6 workflow and panel rubric; peer-review and scientific-critical-thinking procedures; no manuscript prose, spine, ledgers, audit findings, figures, source tables, or project reports.

## Severity and decision rules

- **P0 (must fix before the central claim is publishable):** an unsupported or materially overstated central claim; a circular comparison; a confound that can plausibly generate the primary result; a broken analysis population/denominator; causal wording unsupported by the design; or a fatal mismatch between manuscript, locked spine, and primary evidence. A validated P0 rules out simulated acceptance.
- **P1 (should fix):** an important limitation, alternative explanation, sensitivity failure, reporting omission, or narrative imbalance that changes how a central result should be interpreted but can be repaired by bounded analysis, re-framing, disclosure, or claim narrowing.
- **P2 (polish):** a localized clarity, presentation, or reproducibility improvement that does not materially alter the conclusion.
- A missing report is not evidence that an analysis was not done. A negative or inconclusive result is not evidence for equivalence. Every issue must name an exact location, evidence trail, strongest author counterargument, and the least burdensome adequate remedy—or state why no remedy exists within the present data.
- Simulated decisions use `accept`, `minor revision`, `major revision`, or `reject` only as panel advice; the human/editor retains the decision.

## Precommitted hostile-review criteria

| ID | Criterion | What I will try to falsify | P0 trigger | P1 trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DA1 | Central inference and spine fidelity | Whether the rendered thesis, title, abstract, Results, and Discussion all make the same bounded inference as the locked spine and approved claim ledger | The manuscript upgrades relative predictive support into proof of a uniquely identified mechanism or contradicts the spine | Emphasis, causal verbs, or concluding language outrun the registered evidence while remaining repairable by narrowing |
| DA2 | Independence from circularity | Whether model selection, parameter tuning, gate revision, and evaluation use genuinely held-out information and whether post-outcome amendments are explicitly separated | The same outcome data determine a model/test and then serve as its claimed independent confirmation | Amendment chronology or leakage boundaries are unclear enough to inflate apparent confirmation |
| DA3 | Model completeness and boring alternatives | Whether integration wins only against an impoverished extrema comparator, while unmodelled lapses, engagement, urgency/leak, sensory compression, bias, or correlated noise could explain the advantage | A plausible, already evidenced alternative invalidates the primary comparison and is ignored | Comparator scope or known misfit is under-described, making relative support look like exhaustive mechanism identification |
| DA4 | Out-of-condition predictive generalization | Whether the frozen control-fit model predicts pulse-trial choice and response time without refitting, and whether failures are reported as failures rather than salvaged through secondary fits | The claimed generalization relies on post-pulse refitting, changed estimands, or selective outcome/window choice | The transfer test is legitimate but its failure, low sensitivity, or revised-gate history is narratively softened |
| DA5 | Stimulus replay/counterfactual validity | Whether pulse and control stimuli support the claimed counterfactual when replay noise is redrawn after pulse offset, physical omission is limited, or motion-energy reconstruction is incomplete | The causal interpretation requires a no-pulse counterfactual that the stimulus construction cannot supply | Replay uncertainty is real and bounded but not made prominent at the exact claims/figures it limits |
| DA6 | Pulse causal interpretation and controls | Whether randomized pulse assignment supports the stated causal effect after exclusions, site/schedule asymmetries, no-response mislabelling, energy calibration uncertainty, and response-time selection | Outcome-dependent exclusions, label corruption, or missing controls plausibly reverse or create the central pulse effect without adequate sensitivity analysis | The average pulse effect is credible, but timing/mechanistic interpretation or cross-site generalization is too strong |
| DA7 | Sensitivity, precision, and null logic | Whether failed or inconclusive checks have enough power/sensitivity for the claims attached to them, with intervals and scientific margins rather than p-value logic | A central claim treats an insensitive test as exclusion/equivalence or as independent confirmation | Low sensitivity is disclosed but insufficiently propagated into abstract, figure framing, or Discussion conclusions |
| DA8 | Units, denominators, exclusions, and multiplicity | Whether group claims use mouse-level units; denominators are consistent across prose, legends, tables, and ledgers; missing/excluded trials are traceable; multiplicity is handled | Inflated units, incompatible populations, or undisclosed outcome-dependent exclusions undermine a central result | Denominator/multiplicity reporting is incomplete but the underlying result remains interpretable |
| DA9 | Figure and source-data adversarial consistency | Whether every visual comparison matches its adjacent source table, axes/uncertainty/units are explicit, and salient plots do not conceal heterogeneity or known misfit | Figure/source mismatch, selective display, or pooled presentation materially misrepresents a central result | Display choices obscure mouse-level heterogeneity, negative checks, or analysis-population differences |
| DA10 | Hidden controls and confounds | Whether training, lab/site, rig, coherence schedule, screen geometry, motor/choice bias, reaction-time censoring, and engagement covary with the tested manipulation/model contrast | An uncontrolled factor is structurally confounded with the central condition and no within-factor evidence can separate it | A plausible confound remains but can be bounded by existing stratification, sensitivity analysis, or limitation text |
| DA11 | Narrative balance and negative-model-check weighting | Whether the paper is scientifically coherent rather than organized around repeated failures; whether negative checks illuminate the positive claim instead of becoming a substitute for positive evidence | The central paper claim depends mainly on failures of alternatives or failed tests, with no direct positive evidence at the claimed scope | Negative/inconclusive model checks receive disproportionate space or rhetorical weight relative to the robust behavioral/model-selection results |
| DA12 | Reproducibility and integrity trail | Whether approved claims and numbers trace to ledgers and primary outputs; Methods match Results; audit caveats are visible; missing ethics/data/code facts are not papered over | A central number/claim lacks a source, a known integrity failure is unresolved, or required methods are invented | Reproducibility/reporting gaps remain but can be fixed without changing the analysis |
| DA13 | Sink-the-paper experiment | What feasible result or control would reverse the interpretation, and whether an existing analysis already approximates that falsification | Existing evidence supplies the falsifier and the manuscript nevertheless asserts the refuted thesis | The falsifier is not available; the manuscript should state the decisive future test and limit present claims accordingly |
| DA14 | eLife significance and editorial risk | Whether the contribution is broad and conceptually clean enough for eLife once qualifications are stated, rather than a technically careful but narrow model-comparison report | Correctly bounded claims leave no substantial advance or the main conclusion is not supportable | The advance survives but needs sharper scope, significance, or separation of primary from exploratory evidence |

## Required report structure

The final report will include: a working-draft disclosure and review limits; a neutral summary and genuine strengths; a simulated decision with one-sentence reason; numbered P0/P1/P2 issues; exact manuscript and evidence locations; the strongest reasonable author counterargument to each issue; a bounded remedy or an explicit statement that no remedy exists in the current data; a short “what would sink the paper” test; and counts by severity. I will not edit the manuscript, spine, ledgers, state, or advance through G5.
