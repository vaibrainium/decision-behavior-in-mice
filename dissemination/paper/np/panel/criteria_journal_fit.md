# Criteria commitment: journal fit (S6)

Sources used before opening manuscript content: neuropaper S6 workflow, reviewer role, review rubric and
formats; peer-review, scientific-writing and venue-template skills; current public eLife author/process pages
checked 2026-10-26. No manuscript, draft, spine, section brief, figure legend, ledger, audit or project config
was opened to construct these criteria.

Venue frame: provisional eLife Research Article / Reviewed Preprint pathway, with a second-use requirement as
a thesis chapter. Current eLife selects submissions for peer review, then publishes every reviewed paper as a
Reviewed Preprint with an eLife Assessment and public reviews; it does not make an accept/reject decision after
review. Accordingly, this review will separately state (i) likely editorial selection for peer review and
(ii) likely significance/evidence framing. The neuropaper-required accept/minor/major/reject label will be
explicitly marked as internal simulated shorthand, not represented as an actual eLife decision.

Severity: P0 = submission- or appraisal-blocking defect, unsupported central framing, logic break, or missing
required element; P1 = material venue-fit, structure, accessibility, or reporting problem that should be fixed;
P2 = optional polish. Any issue will give an exact location and the least burdensome adequate remedy. Missing
author-supplied facts will be separated from scientific revision and will not be treated as evidence that the
science itself needs reanalysis.

| ID | Criterion | What I will look for | BLOCK / P0 if | REVISE / P1 if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JF1 | eLife scope and editorial-selection case | A life-science question with a clear reason that expert public review would be valuable; fit is judged by scientific content, not prestige language. | The work is outside eLife's life-science scope, or the manuscript provides no coherent research question/contribution that an editor could send for expert review. | Scope is plausible but the broad relevance, field conflict, or value of review is buried or framed only for a narrow insider audience. |
| JF2 | Significance and evidence are calibrated separately | The title, abstract, opening and close distinguish importance from strength of support and allow a likely eLife-style significance/evidence characterization (useful-to-landmark; inadequate-to-exceptional) without overstating either. | A central title/abstract claim exceeds the reported design/evidence, treats an inconclusive or failed test as confirmation/equivalence, or obscures a result that reverses the advertised contribution. | Importance is inflated, undersold, or vague; evidence qualifications appear too late; the likely assessment would be avoidably weaker because framing is imprecise. |
| JF3 | Title, abstract and impact entry point | The title is intelligible and evidence-matched; the abstract states question, approach/system, scale where essential, principal positive and limiting results, and bounded conclusion; the opening supplies an editor-readable impact case. | The title/abstract materially misrepresents the main result, omits a central failed/inconclusive result needed to interpret the contribution, or introduces an unsupported claim. | The title is jargon-heavy or diffuse, the abstract lacks a clear result/limitation, or non-specialists cannot tell why the study matters by the end of the opening. |
| JF4 | Narrative accessibility and logical arc | A non-specialist life-science editor can follow challenge -> competing account/prediction -> result -> limitation -> conclusion; terms are defined, transitions expose why each analysis/figure is needed, and thesis-chapter detail does not swamp the article arc. | The central inference cannot be reconstructed, sections contradict one another, or essential methods/results are inaccessible without undocumented external context. | The arc is discernible but delayed by dense jargon, repeated setup, unexplained model names, weak transitions, or thesis-style digressions. |
| JF5 | Length and dual-use discipline | Main text, Methods, legends and supplements are proportionate to the claim set; article-critical material stays in the article, while thesis-expansion material is visibly separable. Counts will be reported by section rather than judged from a generic hard limit unless current official instructions specify one. | Essential support is displaced out of the reviewable manuscript, or length/organization makes the central argument effectively unauditable. | Redundancy, long methodological detours, repeated caveats, or thesis exposition creates a credible editorial burden that can be fixed by bounded cuts/moves. |
| JF6 | Figure and supplement burden | Each main figure advances one necessary claim, panel order matches the text, legends make figures appraisable, and supplementary items support rather than rescue the main story. The number/density of displays is weighed against the manuscript's claims and editor readability. | A central claim depends on an absent/unavailable display, or the display package cannot support appraisal of the advertised contribution. | Main figures are overcrowded, figure order obscures the story, redundant panels dilute the contribution, or a crucial caveat/control is relegated too far from the relevant claim. |
| JF7 | Front matter and submission package | The draft distinguishes manuscript content from submission metadata and identifies all required/provisionally required elements: title, abstract, authors/affiliations, corresponding-author information, preprint status/DOI, subject area, ethics, funding, competing interests, contributions, acknowledgments, data/code/materials availability, source data, and any AI-use disclosure governed by current policy. | A required scientific/ethical declaration is silently absent or contradicted, or the manuscript falsely asserts an unverified approval, author fact, availability status, or disclosure. | Required fields remain explicit placeholders, source-data/code availability is not yet actionable, or article elements such as an accessible digest are not planned/clearly separated for venue preparation. |
| JF8 | Reporting completeness without boilerplate | Design, units, allocation/randomization/masking, exclusions, sample-size rationale, analysis populations, uncertainty, software/model provenance, and data/code/source-data routes are locatable or explicitly marked missing/not applicable. | A missing reporting element prevents evaluation of a central claim, or a placeholder masks an unknown scientific fact that could change interpretation. | Reporting is incomplete but can be repaired from existing records without new scientific work; details are scattered enough to impede appraisal. |
| JF9 | Author-fact blockers versus scientific revision | Every unresolved item is classified as (A) submission-blocking author/administrative fact, (B) scientific reporting revision using existing evidence, or (C) new analysis/experiment. Remedies will not silently convert A into C. | The package could be submitted with fabricated or assumed authorship, ethics, funding, conflict, availability, permission, or institutional facts. | Author facts are clearly marked but not consolidated into a bounded completion list, or scientific/editorial issues are mixed with administrative prerequisites. |
| JF10 | Transparent-review and preprint readiness | The manuscript can withstand public review: failed, null and inconclusive tests are prominent; limitations and alternative explanations are explicit; any preprint requirement and open-review implications are recognized; source-data expectations are addressed. | The public record would materially mislead because a known failed/inconclusive result, analysis deviation, or decisive limitation is hidden. | Transparency is present but dispersed, euphemistic, or inconsistent; preprint/source-data readiness is not yet documented. |
| JF11 | Thesis-chapter compatibility without weakening journal fit | The core manuscript stands alone as an article, while thesis-specific context, historical detail, or extended methodological teaching can be retained in a clearly separable layer. | Thesis dependence leaves the journal manuscript incomplete or introduces claims outside the locked scientific scope. | Dual-use prose causes repetition, overlong background, or inconsistent audience/voice; a bounded move to thesis-only context would sharpen the article. |
| JF12 | Proportionate and bounded remedies | Every issue is anchored to a heading/paragraph/figure/table/placeholder and asks for clarification, reordering, deletion, a narrow addition, or explicit limitation before asking for new work. | A central claim cannot be supported without a new analysis/experiment and cannot be narrowed honestly; this will be named as a scientific blocker, not disguised as polish. | The claim can stand after a bounded textual, structural, front-matter, or display remedy using existing verified material. |

## Decision mapping committed in advance

- **Likely selected for eLife peer review:** scope is clear, public expert review would add value, and no P0
  blocks appraisal. **Unlikely selected:** the contribution is too unclear/narrow for the stated framing or a P0
  prevents credible triage. This is a simulation, not an editorial decision.
- **Internal accept shorthand:** zero P0 and at most optional P2 items.
- **Internal minor revision:** zero P0 and only bounded P1/P2 editorial or reporting repairs.
- **Internal major revision:** one or more P0s or several linked P1s materially weaken selection/readability but
  the paper can be repaired without replacing its core study.
- **Internal reject shorthand:** fatal scope/logic mismatch or the central claim cannot survive bounded narrowing;
  this is not an actual post-review eLife outcome.

## Review limits committed in advance

This is a journal-fit review, not a substitute for the parallel statistics, domain, devil's-advocate or integrity
audits. I will inspect those artifacts only to understand already identified blockers and will not relitigate their
specialist judgments. I will not request new analyses merely to increase perceived impact, edit manuscript/ledgers/
state, or cross G5. The final report is a working simulated assessment requiring accountable human verification.
