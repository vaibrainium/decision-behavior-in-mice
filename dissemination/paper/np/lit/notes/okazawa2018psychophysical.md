# okazawa2018psychophysical

Okazawa G, Sha L, Purcell BA, Kiani R (2018). *Psychophysical reverse correlation reflects both sensory and decision-making processes*. Nature Communications. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-018-05797-y; PMID: 30154467. Access: full author PDF and publisher article; metadata cross-checked in PubMed.

- **Question / preparation:** Which sensory, decision and motor processes shape psychophysical temporal kernels?
- **Key findings:** Sensory/motor delays, decision dynamics and bounds all distorted reverse-correlation kernels. Locator: Results model analyses; Discussion.
- **Relevance:** Methods caveat for RT-conditioned or pulse-aligned timing summaries.
- **Limitations for our use:** Kernel distortion does not specify which component causes the present model mismatch.
- **Paraphrase for C006/C007:** A behavioural temporal kernel is a composite of sensory, decision and response processes.
