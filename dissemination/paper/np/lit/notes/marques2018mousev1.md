# marques2018mousev1

Marques T, Nguyen J, Fioreze G, Petreanu L (2018). *A Role for Mouse Primary Visual Cortex in Motion Perception*. Current Biology. DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2018.04.012; PMID: 29779878. Access: PubMed abstract and figure text.

- **Question / species / task:** Head-fixed mice reported RDK motion direction in a two-alternative task.
- **Key finding:** Mice reached high accuracy at strong coherence; accuracy increased with stimulus duration and visual-field coverage, consistent with temporal and spatial integration. Locator: Abstract; Fig. 1 task timeline.
- **Relevance:** Closest prior continuous-RDK mouse decision task.
- **Limitation for our claim:** The stimulus was fixed-duration and responses followed offset; no randomized motion pulse, joint free-response choice/RT likelihood, or frozen out-of-condition prediction.
