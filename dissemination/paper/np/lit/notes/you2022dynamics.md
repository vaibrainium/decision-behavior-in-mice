# you2022dynamics

You W-K, Mysore SP (2022). *Dynamics of Visual Perceptual Decision-Making in Freely Behaving Mice*. eNeuro. DOI: 10.1523/ENEURO.0161-21.2022; PMID: 35273103; PMCID: PMC8925649. Access: PMC full text.

- **Question / species / task:** Freely moving mice performed touchscreen 2AFC orientation discrimination with target size, luminance, duration and distractors varied.
- **Key findings:** Conditional accuracy and RT distributions supported an early sensory-encoding phase; informative stimuli could be very brief. Locator: Abstract; Fig. 1 task description; conditional-accuracy Results.
- **Relevance:** Mouse visual decision and response-time comparator.
- **Limitation for our claim:** Static gratings rather than continuous RDK; no signed pulse perturbation and no generative joint choice/RT transfer model.
