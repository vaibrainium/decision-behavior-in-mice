# stirman2016touchscreen

Stirman J, Townsend LB, Smith S (2016). *A touchscreen based global motion perception task for mice*. Vision Research. DOI: 10.1016/j.visres.2016.07.006; PMID: 27497283; PMCID: PMC5035629. Access: PubMed abstract / PMC full text.

- **Question / species / task:** Mice; touchscreen two-alternative RDK global-motion discrimination.
- **Key finding:** Mice learned the task and showed coherence thresholds; dot size, lifetime and speed were manipulated. Locator: Abstract; Methods.
- **Relevance:** Direct mouse continuous-RDK decision-task precedent.
- **Limitation for our claim:** Fixed task conditions rather than a brief pulse embedded in continuous motion; no joint choice/RT model or frozen transfer test.
