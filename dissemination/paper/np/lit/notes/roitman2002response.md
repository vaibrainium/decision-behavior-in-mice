# roitman2002response

**Citation:** Roitman JD, Shadlen MN. *Response of neurons in the lateral intraparietal area during a combined visual discrimination reaction time task.* The Journal of Neuroscience. 2002;22:9475–9489. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.22-21-09475.2002. PMID: 12417672. Access: PMC/PubMed abstract.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Rhesus macaques performed two-choice random-dot direction discrimination with saccadic reports; motion strength varied. Behavioural choice and RT were measured while recording lateral intraparietal cortex (LIP).

**Key findings:** Accuracy and decision speed increased with motion strength, and LIP activity during decision formation changed in ways predictive of the upcoming saccade (abstract).

**Relevance:** Primary task precedent for jointly considering choice and RT in reaction-time random-dot decisions; supports the methodological rationale around C002/C004, not mouse-specific biology.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Neural recordings and saccadic reports in macaques differ from the present mouse response apparatus; abstract-level review does not establish identical nondecision components.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Reaction-time random-dot tasks provide coupled psychometric and chronometric constraints while allowing the decision interval to be defined from motion onset to response (abstract).
