# zhou2009time

Zhou X, Wong-Lin K, Philip H (2009). *Time-varying perturbations can distinguish among integrate-to-threshold models for perceptual decision making in reaction time tasks*. Neural Computation. DOI: 10.1162/neco.2009.07-08-817; PMID: 19416080. Access: full manuscript (arXiv:0901.2173); published metadata cross-checked in PubMed.

- **Question / method:** Which perturbation-dependent choice and RT summaries distinguish integrate-to-threshold model classes?
- **Key findings:** Time-dependent effects on RT distributions/moments can separate some candidate models, but sensory noise and delays reduce separability. Locator: Results simulations; Discussion.
- **Relevance:** Direct methods precedent for treating a pulse as a joint-distribution model test rather than only a choice shift.
- **Limitations for our use:** Theoretical/simulation study; discrimination must be recalibrated at the actual schedules, noise and sample sizes.
- **Paraphrase for C006/C007:** Perturbations are informative only when the candidate predictions remain separable under realistic observation noise and delay.
