# wong2007neural

**Citation:** Wong KF, Huk AC, Shadlen MN, Wang XJ. *Neural circuit dynamics underlying accumulation of time-varying evidence during perceptual decision making.* Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience. 2007;1:6. DOI: 10.3389/neuro.10.006.2007. PMID: 18946528. Access: PubMed abstract; PMC full text.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** A recurrent attractor model simulated macaque LIP, choice and response-time effects of brief motion pulses.

**Key findings:** The model reproduced persistent neural/behavioural pulse effects and weaker effects of later pulses, while predicting nonlinear paired-pulse interactions (PubMed abstract).

**Relevance:** Shows that pulse persistence and timing can be generated by nonlinear recurrent dynamics rather than uniquely by a simple DDM.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Model-based interpretation of macaque results; paired-pulse predictions were not tested in the present dataset.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Recurrent-attractor dynamics can reproduce persistent and timing-dependent motion-pulse effects and predict nonadditive interactions (abstract).
