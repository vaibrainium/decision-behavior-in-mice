# yates2017functional

**Citation:** Yates JL, Park IM, Katz LN, Pillow JW, Huk AC. *Functional dissection of signal and noise in MT and LIP during decision-making.* Nature Neuroscience. 2017;20:1285–1292. DOI: 10.1038/nn.4611. PMID: 28758998. Access: PubMed abstract and figures; PMC full text available.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Simultaneous MT and LIP recordings in macaques performing a time-varying motion task separated sensory encoding from downstream choice-related dynamics.

**Key findings:** Early behavioural weighting was already present in MT responses and was recapitulated in LIP; a model observer that perfectly integrated realistic MT output inherited early weighting (PubMed abstract; Figure 4).

**Relevance:** Strong sensory-level alternative under H3: temporal weighting can arise upstream of the decision integrator.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Macaque MT/LIP and a different pulsed stimulus; does not establish mouse visual dynamics or explain the present late response-associated contrast.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Time-dependent sensory responses can generate early behavioural weighting even when downstream modeled integration is perfect (abstract; Figure 4).
