# ratcliff2008diffusion

**Citation:** Ratcliff R, McKoon G. *The diffusion decision model: theory and data for two-choice decision tasks.* Neural Computation. 2008;20:873–922. DOI: 10.1162/neco.2008.12-06-420. PMID: 18085991. Access: PubMed abstract; PMC full text available.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Review and demonstrations across human two-choice tasks. The diffusion model relates accuracy, mean RT and full RT distributions to drift, boundary, bias and nondecision processes; no single neural region is the target.

**Key findings:** The review describes how stimulus difficulty, speed/accuracy instructions and stimulus proportions affect separable model components, and emphasizes empirical constraints across accuracy and RT distributions (abstract).

**Relevance:** Core modelling precedent for C004 and for diagnosing a model with both choices and response-time distributions rather than psychometric fit alone.

**Limitations stated/visible:** General review; it does not prescribe this project's pulse input, mixture component or censoring implementation.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Diffusion models are constrained jointly by accuracy and response-time distributions, allowing experimental manipulations to be mapped onto distinct latent components (abstract).
