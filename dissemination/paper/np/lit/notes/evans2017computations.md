# evans2017computations

**Citation:** Evans NJ, Hawkins GE, Boehm U, Wagenmakers EJ, Brown SD. *The computations that support simple decision-making: A comparison between the diffusion and urgency-gating models.* Scientific Reports. 2017;7:16433. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-017-16694-7. PMID: 29180789. Access: PubMed abstract; PMC full text.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Human behavioural experiments and formal qualitative/quantitative predictions compared diffusion with urgency gating under speeded decisions.

**Key findings:** Diffusion predicted the reported behaviour better than urgency gating, including lasting influence of early information (PubMed abstract; PMC Results/Discussion).

**Relevance:** Counterbalances urgency evidence and supports retaining the accumulator as the preferred tested control model without claiming uniqueness.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Human task and specific UGM/DDM implementations; no validation of the current replay or pulse likelihood.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Matched qualitative and quantitative tests can favor diffusion over urgency gating, including evidence that early information retains influence (abstract and Discussion).
