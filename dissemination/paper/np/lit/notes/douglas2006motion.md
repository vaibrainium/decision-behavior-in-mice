# douglas2006motion

Douglas RM, Neve A, Quittenbaum JP, Alam NM, Prusky GT (2006). *Perception of visual motion coherence by rats and mice*. Vision Research. DOI: 10.1016/j.visres.2006.02.025; PMID: 16647739. Access: PubMed abstract.

- **Question / species / task:** Rats and mice; direction discrimination in random-dot kinematograms.
- **Key finding:** Mice and rats could discriminate global motion in noisy RDKs, with average rodent coherence thresholds around 25%. Locator: PubMed Abstract.
- **Relevance:** Early mouse RDK psychophysics; rejects novelty at the level of mouse global-motion discrimination.
- **Limitation for our claim:** No brief randomized perturbation, response-time accumulation model, or held-out pulse prediction.
