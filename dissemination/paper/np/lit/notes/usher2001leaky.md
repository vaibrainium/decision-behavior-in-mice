# usher2001leaky

**Citation:** Usher M, McClelland JL. 2001. The time course of perceptual choice: the leaky, competing accumulator model. *Psychological Review* 108:550–592. DOI: 10.1037/0033-295X.108.3.550. PMID: 11488378.

**Access:** abstract only, PubMed. **Question:** Can leaky, stochastic and mutually inhibitory accumulators explain perceptual-choice timing? **Method:** nonlinear decision-unit model evaluated against time-controlled and reaction-time behaviour.

**Key findings:** The abstract describes diffusion as a special case and argues that leakage plus competition address benchmarks not captured by simpler diffusion/random-walk formulations.

**Relevance:** canonical alternative showing that leak and competition jointly shape temporal weighting. **Limitation:** the present rapid-forgetting approximation is not the full LCA and must not be labelled an exact test of this model.
