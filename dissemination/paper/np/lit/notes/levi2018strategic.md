# levi2018strategic

Levi AJ, Yates JL, Huk AC, Katz LN (2018). *Strategic and Dynamic Temporal Weighting for Perceptual Decisions in Humans and Macaques*. eNeuro. DOI: 10.1523/ENEURO.0169-18.2018; PMID: 30406190. Access: PubMed abstract; open full text available in PMC6220584.

- **Question / preparation:** Can humans and macaques adapt temporal weights to the statistics of time-varying sensory evidence?
- **Key findings:** Both species adjusted temporal weighting strategically when stimulus statistics changed. Locator: PubMed Abstract.
- **Relevance:** Shows that weighting is task-dependent rather than a fixed fingerprint of one accumulator.
- **Limitations for our use:** Different explicit temporal-statistics manipulation; abstract-level appraisal.
- **Paraphrase for C001/C007:** Temporal weights can change strategically with stimulus statistics.
