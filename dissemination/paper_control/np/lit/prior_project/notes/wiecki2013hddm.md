# wiecki2013hddm

**Citation:** Wiecki TV, Sofer I, Frank MJ. *HDDM: Hierarchical Bayesian estimation of the Drift-Diffusion Model in Python.* Frontiers in Neuroinformatics. 2013;7:14. DOI: 10.3389/fninf.2013.00014. PMID: 23935581. Access: PubMed abstract; PMC full text available.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Methodological paper introducing hierarchical Bayesian estimation for diffusion models, demonstrated on behavioural datasets; not tied to a single species, motion task or neural region.

**Key findings:** Hierarchical estimation pools information while retaining subject-level parameters and the paper evaluates parameter recovery and model comparison (abstract).

**Relevance:** General precedent for recovery and hierarchical diffusion fitting. It is not the fitting software or inferential approach used in the present study and should not be cited as if it were.

**Limitations stated/visible:** The implementation and priors differ from the present maximum-likelihood/CV pipeline; relevance is conceptual only.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Diffusion-model parameters require explicit recovery assessment, especially when subject-level data are limited (abstract).
