# tsetsos2012using

Tsetsos K, Gao J, McClelland JL, Usher M (2012). *Using Time-Varying Evidence to Test Models of Decision Dynamics: Bounded Diffusion vs. the Leaky Competing Accumulator Model*. Frontiers in Neuroscience. DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2012.00079; PMID: 22701399. Access: full text via Europe PMC (PMC3372959), Methods and Discussion.

- **Question / preparation:** Can temporal weighting under time-varying evidence distinguish bounded diffusion from leaky competing accumulation in humans?
- **Key findings:** Temporal weighting depended on experimental conditions, and primacy-like patterns could arise from competing mechanisms; some diagnostic patterns appeared only in subsets of observers. Locator: Results temporal-weight analyses; Discussion.
- **Relevance:** Closest formal pulse/weighting comparator and direct warning against mapping a kernel shape to one mechanism.
- **Limitations for our use:** Human task and different evidence schedule; individual heterogeneity limits pooled mechanistic inference.
- **Paraphrase for C007:** A temporal weighting pattern is not a one-to-one signature of a specific accumulator dynamic.
