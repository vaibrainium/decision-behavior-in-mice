# samonds2018motion

Samonds JM et al. (2018). *Motion Discrimination and the Motion Aftereffect in Mouse Vision*. eNeuro. DOI: 10.1523/ENEURO.0065-18.2018; PMID: 30627645; PMCID: PMC6325549. Access: PMC full text.

- **Question / species / task:** Head-fixed mice learned a conditioned-lick RDK direction discrimination and were tested for a motion aftereffect.
- **Key findings:** Lick count and latency varied with coherence; prolonged coherent adapters shifted responses to subsequent zero-coherence motion. Locator: Methods, Training paradigm; Results Figs. 3–4.
- **Relevance:** Mouse dynamic RDK with response latency and a preceding stimulus manipulation.
- **Limitation for our claim:** The adapter was prolonged and condition-based rather than a brief randomized signed pulse; analyses were fitted curves, not a frozen joint choice/RT model tested out of condition.
