# chakravarty2025cross

Chakravarty S, Delgado-Sallent C, Kane GA, Xia H, Do QH, Senne RA, Scott BB (2025). *A cross-species framework for investigating perceptual evidence accumulation*. eLife reviewed preprint. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.105116.1. Access: full publisher reviewed-preprint text; metadata from eLife (published 2025-04-11). **Preprint/reviewed preprint; not a version of record.**

- **Question / preparation:** How do mice, rats and humans perform a shared pulse-based evidence task, and which strategy models describe cross-species behaviour?
- **Key findings:** The framework uses repeated discrete evidence pulses and models time pressure/strategy changes; the authors report evidence that mice may switch strategies. Locator: reviewed-preprint Abstract; Results model-comparison sections.
- **Relevance:** Recent cross-species comparator and potential response-policy alternative.
- **Limitations for our use:** Repeated-pulse task rather than a brief perturbation embedded in continuous random-dot motion; preprint status; not an out-of-condition control-to-pulse validation.
- **Paraphrase for C001/C006:** Pulse tasks can reveal species- and policy-dependent strategies, but task design matters.
