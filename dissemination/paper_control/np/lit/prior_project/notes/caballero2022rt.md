# caballero2022rt

Treviño M, Medina-Coss Y León R, Lezama E (2022). *Response Time Distributions and the Accumulation of Visual Evidence in Freely Moving Mice*. Neuroscience. DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroscience.2022.08.015; PMID: 35995337. Access: PubMed abstract.

- **Question / species / task:** Freely moving adult mice performed an automated visual discrimination task while visual-cortical and decision-related regions were pharmacologically manipulated.
- **Key finding:** Choices and escape latencies depended on stimulus discriminability; a DDM fit linked drift rate to stimulus contrast and cortical inactivation. Locator: PubMed Abstract.
- **Relevance:** Closest retained precedent for a mouse visual choice/response-time diffusion model.
- **Limitation for our claim:** Not a continuous-RDK pulse experiment, and no frozen control-fit prediction on randomized pulse trials.
