# kumano2024employment

Kumano H, Uka T (2024). *Employment of time-varying sensory evidence to test the mechanisms underlying flexible decision-making*. NeuroReport. DOI: 10.1097/WNR.0000000000001980; PMID: 38064356. Access: full text via Europe PMC (PMC10766094), Methods, Results and Discussion.

- **Question / preparation:** How do relevant and irrelevant time-varying motion pulses influence flexible decisions in a task-switching context?
- **Key findings:** Relevant and irrelevant pulse effects had different temporal profiles. Locator: Results pulse-effect analyses; Discussion.
- **Relevance:** Closest published paper in the recency sweep; demonstrates that specified task contrasts give pulses mechanistic value.
- **Limitations for our use:** Task switching, pooled analyses and neural context differ; its temporal profiles cannot be imported into the present mouse task.
- **Paraphrase for C005/C006:** Time-varying evidence can reveal task-dependent processing when competing conditions make distinct predictions.
