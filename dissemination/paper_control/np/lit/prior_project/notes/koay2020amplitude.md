# koay2020amplitude

Koay SA et al. (2020). *Amplitude modulations of cortical sensory responses in pulsatile evidence accumulation*. eLife. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.60628; PMID: 33263278; PMCID: PMC7811404. Access: PMC full text.

- **Question / species / task:** Mouse posterior-cortical responses during the VR accumulating-towers task.
- **Key findings:** Randomized, well-separated 200-ms visual cues permitted cue-triggered neural response measurement during an unfolding decision. Locator: Abstract; Behavioral task Methods.
- **Relevance:** Mouse neural study of dynamic, randomized visual evidence pulses.
- **Limitation for our claim:** Towers comprised the evidence stream rather than perturbing continuous RDK; no control-trained joint choice/RT model or held-out pulse transfer test.
