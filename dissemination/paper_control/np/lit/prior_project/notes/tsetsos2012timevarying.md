# tsetsos2012timevarying

**Citation:** Tsetsos K, Gao J, McClelland JL, Usher M. *Using Time-Varying Evidence to Test Models of Decision Dynamics: Bounded Diffusion vs. the Leaky Competing Accumulator Model.* Frontiers in Neuroscience. 2012;6:79. DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2012.00079. PMID: 22701399. Access: PubMed abstract and figure captions; PMC full text available.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Human observers received pulse and other time-varying motion evidence. Behaviour and simulations compared bounded diffusion with a leaky competing accumulator; no neural recording region.

**Key findings:** The authors show that primacy can arise in a leaky competing accumulator when inhibition dominates leak and that task time pressure changes temporal weighting (abstract). Pulse perturbations shifted psychometric functions, with effectiveness varying by pulse time (Figure 6 caption).

**Relevance:** Direct counter-method precedent: temporal pulse effects and primacy do not uniquely identify perfect integration.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Human fixed-duration/time-pressure manipulations; model architecture differs from the present rapid-forgetting approximation.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Similar temporal-weighting signatures can be generated by bounded diffusion or leaky competition, so pulse timing must be evaluated with model-specific sensitivity (abstract).
