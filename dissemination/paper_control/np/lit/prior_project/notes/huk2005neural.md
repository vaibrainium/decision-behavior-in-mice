# huk2005neural

Huk AC, Shadlen MN (2005). *Neural activity in macaque parietal cortex reflects temporal integration of visual motion signals during perceptual decision making*. Journal of Neuroscience. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4684-04.2005; PMID: 16280581. Access: full author PDF (Methods and Results); metadata cross-checked in PubMed.

- **Question / preparation:** How do brief motion perturbations alter decisions and LIP activity in macaques performing reaction-time random-dot motion discrimination?
- **Key findings:** Brief pulses affected choices, response times and LIP activity beyond the pulse duration; bounded integration accounted for part of the decline in late pulse efficacy. Locator: Abstract; Results, pulse perturbation analyses.
- **Relevance:** Closest empirical precedent. Supports using joint choice/RT consequences, but conflicts with any inference that a delayed behavioural response alone measures when evidence was integrated.
- **Limitations for our use:** Macaque task plus neural recordings; the neural persistence evidence cannot be imported into mouse behaviour.
- **Paraphrase for C001/C006:** Motion pulses can probe accumulation, but their behavioural timing must be interpreted together with commitment and response dynamics.
