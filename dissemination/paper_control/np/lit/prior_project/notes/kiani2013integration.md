# kiani2013integration

Kiani R, Churchland AK, Shadlen MN (2013). *Integration of direction cues is invariant to the temporal gap between them*. Journal of Neuroscience. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2094-13.2013; PMID: 24133253. Access: full author PDF; metadata cross-checked in PubMed.

- **Question / preparation:** Do humans retain and combine two motion samples separated by variable evidence-free gaps?
- **Key findings:** Observers combined the direction cues across variable gaps, placing constraints on retention dynamics. Locator: Results, gap-duration analyses; Discussion.
- **Relevance:** Establishes that retention can be tested with an independently manipulated gap; this differs from inferring retention from post-pulse response latency.
- **Limitations for our use:** Human fixed-duration two-sample task; the present data do not independently manipulate a blank gap.
- **Paraphrase for C007:** Evidence retention and pulse-onset weighting are experimentally distinct questions.
