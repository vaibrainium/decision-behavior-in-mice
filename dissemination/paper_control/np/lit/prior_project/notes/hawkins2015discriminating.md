# hawkins2015discriminating

**Citation:** Hawkins GE, Wagenmakers EJ, Ratcliff R, Brown SD. *Discriminating evidence accumulation from urgency signals in speeded decision making.* Journal of Neurophysiology. 2015;114:40–47. DOI: 10.1152/jn.00088.2015. PMID: 25904706. Access: PubMed abstract; PMC full text.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Simulations and two behavioural datasets tested whether evidence-accumulation and urgency-gating models are mathematically or empirically distinguishable.

**Key findings:** The models were distinguishable in realistic designs; accumulation fit one dataset best and urgency gating another (PubMed abstract; PMC Discussion).

**Relevance:** Demonstrates both model mimicry and the value of task-specific predictive discrimination rather than assuming one universal mechanism.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Two datasets and model implementations; successful discrimination elsewhere does not guarantee sensitivity in this experiment.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Accumulation and urgency models can be distinguished in suitable designs, but the preferred model can vary across datasets (abstract).
