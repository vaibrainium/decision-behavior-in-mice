# kiani2008bounded

Kiani R, Hanks TD, Shadlen MN (2008). *Bounded integration in parietal cortex underlies decisions even when viewing duration is dictated by the environment*. Journal of Neuroscience. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4761-07.2008; PMID: 18354005. Access: PubMed abstract; open full text available in PMC6670720.

- **Question / preparation:** Do observers and LIP neurons stop incorporating motion evidence after a decision bound is reached even when viewing continues?
- **Key findings:** Behavioural and neural results supported bounded accumulation and commitment before stimulus termination. Locator: PubMed Abstract.
- **Relevance:** A late pulse can be ineffective because a bound has already been crossed, not because the accumulator necessarily leaks.
- **Limitations for our use:** Fixed-duration macaque task with physiology; it does not test held-out pulse generalization.
- **Paraphrase for C006/C007:** Pulse timing is jointly shaped by evidence retention and the distribution of decision times.
