# parespujolras2025glimpses

Parés-Pujolràs E, Geuzebroek AC, O'Connell RG, Kelly SP (2025). *Perceptual glimpses are locally accumulated and globally maintained at distinct processing levels*. bioRxiv. DOI: 10.1101/2025.04.30.651428. Access: Europe PMC abstract and public bioRxiv metadata; open full text available. **Preprint; no published version found in this sweep.**

- **Question / preparation:** How are up to two motion-evidence pulses separated by variable gaps represented behaviourally and in human EEG?
- **Key findings:** Participants used both pulses but underweighted the second and showed no systematic accuracy decline with gap duration; motor beta tracked cumulative evidence across gaps whereas the CPP responded transiently to each pulse. Locator: Europe PMC Abstract.
- **Relevance:** Closest new preprint on separated motion glimpses; it bears on local accumulation versus global maintenance, not control-model generalization.
- **Limitations for our use:** Human EEG, two-pulse fixed-duration design and preprint status; abstract-level appraisal.
- **Paraphrase for C001/C007:** Different neural levels may transiently integrate versus maintain pulse information across gaps.
