# pinto2018visualpulses

Pinto L, Koay SA, Engelhard B, Yoon AM, Deverett B, Thiberge SY, Witten IB, Tank DW, Brody CD (2018). *An Accumulation-of-Evidence Task Using Visual Pulses for Mice Navigating in Virtual Reality*. Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience. DOI: 10.3389/fnbeh.2018.00036; PMID: 29559900; PMCID: PMC5845651. Access: PMC full text.

- **Question / species / task:** Head-fixed mice navigate a VR T-maze and choose the side with more brief visual towers.
- **Key findings:** Towers occurred at random spatial Poisson locations; mice used multiple pulses across the cue region. Logistic and accumulation models quantified choices. Locator: Abstract; Results Figs. 3, 4, 6, 7.
- **Relevance:** Strong counterexample to any claim that visual pulse accumulation had not been demonstrated in mice.
- **Limitation for our claim:** Discrete towers were the evidence stream, not randomized perturbations embedded in continuous RDK; response time/censoring was not jointly modelled and pulse trials were not held out from a control fit.
