# zhou2009timevarying

**Citation:** Zhou X, Wong-Lin K, Holmes P. *Time-varying perturbations can distinguish among integrate-to-threshold models for perceptual decision making in reaction time tasks.* Neural Computation. 2009;21:2336–2362. DOI: 10.1162/neco.2009.07-08-817. PMID: 19416080. Access: PubMed abstract and PMC full text.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Mathematical analysis and simulations tested whether time-varying perturbations and decision-time moments distinguish drift-diffusion, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck and nonlinear integrate-to-threshold dynamics.

**Key findings:** Some candidates were separable at high signal-to-noise, but constant- and time-varying-drift models were not necessarily distinguishable; perturbation effects depended on regime (abstract; Results/Discussion).

**Relevance:** Direct methodological warning for C006–C009: a pulse only identifies mechanisms after dataset-specific discrimination and sensitivity checks.

**Limitations stated/visible:** One-dimensional simulated models and proposed protocols; no guarantee of identifiability under the present trial counts, noise or response mapping.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Time-varying perturbations distinguish some decision dynamics only under specified regimes and may leave other candidate models indistinguishable (abstract).
