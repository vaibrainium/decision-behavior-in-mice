# winkel2014early

Winkel J, Keuken MC, van Maanen L, Wagenmakers EJ, Forstmann BU (2014). *Early evidence affects later decisions: why evidence accumulation is required to explain response time data*. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. DOI: 10.3758/s13423-013-0551-8; PMID: 24395093. Access: PubMed abstract only.

- **Question / preparation:** Can later choice and RT consequences of early evidence distinguish accumulation from urgency gating?
- **Key findings:** Early evidence altered later decisions and response-time structure in a manner the authors argued required accumulation. Locator: PubMed Abstract.
- **Relevance:** Counterweight to urgency/leak accounts and support for joint choice/RT constraints.
- **Limitations for our use:** Abstract-level appraisal and a different human paradigm; it does not validate the present model implementation.
- **Paraphrase for C001/C004:** Response-time consequences can distinguish models that choices alone leave ambiguous.
