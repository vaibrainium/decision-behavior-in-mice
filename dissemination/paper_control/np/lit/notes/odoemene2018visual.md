# odoemene2018visual

Odoemene O, Pisupati S, Nguyen H, Churchland AK (2018). *Visual Evidence Accumulation Guides Decision-Making in Unrestrained Mice*. Journal of Neuroscience. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3478-17.2018; PMID: 30322902; PMCID: PMC6246883. Access: PMC full text.

- **Question / species / task:** Unrestrained mice categorized whether the number of stochastic full-field flashes during one second exceeded a boundary.
- **Key finding:** Each flash lasted 20 ms; psychometric functions and temporal kernels showed use of evidence across the sequence. Locator: Results, “Mice learned to categorize stochastic sequences of visual flashes”; Fig. 1.
- **Relevance:** Direct counterexample to broad novelty for dynamic visual evidence accumulation in mice.
- **Limitation for our claim:** Discrete flashes were the primary evidence in a fixed-duration task, not held-out pulse perturbations of continuous RDK; no joint RT/censoring model.
