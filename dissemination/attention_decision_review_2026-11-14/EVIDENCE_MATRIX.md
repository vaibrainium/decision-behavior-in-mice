# Source-assessed evidence matrix

Assessment depth distinguishes current full-text sections, abstracts, and earlier verified reviews. Implications are proposals, not findings in this dataset.

| Reference | Design / assessment | Source finding | Limit and implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Brunton BW (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1233912) | Rat/human controlled evidence accumulation; abstract; earlier review | Controlled evidence permits quantitative modeling of accumulation and noise components. | Independent discrete samples offer identification advantages over correlated motion energy. **Next:** Separate sensory sample uncertainty from accumulation noise and latent sensitivity. |
| [Cheadle S (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.01.020) | Human sequential evidence task with pupil/fMRI; selected full text: PMC web | Context-dependent gain favors evidence consistent with recent samples and predicts serial weighting. | This is not arbitrary amplification of surprising or strong pulses. **Next:** Require a signed context/congruency prediction before adding adaptive gain. |
| [Murphy PR (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003854) | Human motion discrimination, pupil and hierarchical DDM; selected full text: XML | Baseline pupil state relates to variability in evidence accumulation rate. | Correlational modeling does not uniquely separate between- and within-trial noise mechanisms. **Next:** Attention/arousal literature does not uniformly favor replacing additive drift variability. |
| [Kelly SP (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1523/jneurosci.3355-13.2013) | Human RDK/EEG; selected full text: PMC web | Decision-signal buildup varies with coherence and endogenous attentional indices. | Neural covariation does not specify a unique gain distribution. **Next:** Sensory sensitivity is plausible, but computational parameters alone do not identify attention. |
| [Nunez MD (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmp.2016.03.003) | Human noise/signal task, EEG-informed hierarchical DDM; selected full text: PMC web | Attention-related EEG measures predict drift and preprocessing time and modestly improve held-out prediction. | Error-RT prediction remains poor; neural features provide constraints absent here. **Next:** Test full choice/RT adequacy, not just a better correct-RT or choice fit. |
| [Loughnane GM (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2015.12.049) | Human uncertain target time/location with EEG; abstract | Target-selection signals modulate evidence accumulation onset and rate. | Human target selection is not directly measured in the mouse task. **Next:** Consider onset gating only if its pulse-time and RT signatures are supported. |
| [Ueno T (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00221-024-06862-3) | Human spatial cueing, motion discrimination and EEG; selected full text: XML | Valid cues affect sensory sensitivity and decision-related timing; CPP begins earlier. | Controlled spatial attention differs from uncued mouse motion; some modeling pooled participants. **Next:** The postdecision-delay null does not test variable sensory encoding or accumulation onset. |
| [Duffy JS (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-025-00916-3) | Review; abstract/metadata; discovery only | Reviews the problem of assigning decision variability to specific processes. | Review synthesis is not a direct test of the proposed models. **Next:** Maintain separate sensory, policy and response hypotheses. |
| [Urai AE. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2025.06.003) | Review; abstract/metadata; discovery only | Reviews temporal structure in perceptual decision variability. | Not primary evidence for this dataset or a new mechanism. **Next:** Use for citation chaining; do not count as an independent experiment. |
| [Ashwood ZC (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-01007-z) | Mouse/human choice GLM-HMM with held-out comparisons; selected full text: author manuscript PDF | Persistent biased and stimulus-guided strategies explain serial choice structure; RT and omissions provide external checks. | State-conditioned RT checks are not a fitted joint choice/RT generative mechanism. **Next:** Compare static history, continuous change and a small HMM; validate pulse/RT consequences separately. |
| [Hulsey D (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2024.113709) | Mouse visual/auditory discrimination, choices, omissions, pupil and movement; selected full text: XML | Persistent performance states covary with arousal and movement; prestimulus measures predict state occupancy. | State labels and physiological associations do not prove attention is the causal variable. **Next:** Assess serial structure and validate inferred states with outcomes excluded from state fitting. |
| [Chakravarty (2025)](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/105116) | Cross-species behavioral accumulation framework; abstract and reviewed-preprint record | Reports mouse behavior better described using multiple strategies in a cross-species evidence task. | Reviewed preprint; task and validation differ from these RDK pulses. **Next:** Constrain novelty to the joint RT/pulse variability problem. |
| [Roy NA (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.12.004) | Dynamic psychophysical models across species; selected full text: author PDF and PMC web | Continuous changes in sensitivity, bias and history weights can be inferred across trials and sessions. | Smoothed trajectories use future data and are not prestimulus causal states. **Next:** Compare continuous dynamics; use filtering or properly masked prediction for pulse tests. |
| [Wyart V (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1523/jneurosci.3276-14.2015) | Human dual-stream categorization with EEG; abstract | Divided attention can impair downstream integration while leaving encoded decision information available. | Two simultaneous streams differ substantially from the mouse single-stream task. **Next:** Attention can affect integration itself; do not reduce the entire literature to sensory gain. |
| [Smith PL (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0015156) | Integrated computational attention/visual memory theory; abstract | Attention changes formation or onset of durable stimulus representations driving decisions. | A theoretical account is not direct validation in these mice. **Next:** Pure postdecision delay and evidence-encoding delay are different model tests. |
| [Goris RL (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3711) | Macaque visual neuronal responses and modulated-Poisson modeling; selected full text: author PDF | Multiplicative excitability fluctuations explain neuronal variability and can have slow temporal correlations. | Includes anesthesia effects and is not a behavioral demonstration of attention. **Next:** Assess gain persistence rather than assuming an iid scalar gain is an attention model. |
| [Calder-Travis J (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmp.2023.102815) | Theoretical fluctuating-stimulus DDM/confidence model; selected full text: XML | Defines trial-to-trial drift-rate scaling multiplicatively for time-varying sensory evidence. | A confidence derivation does not demonstrate that positive gain explains mouse pulse/RT data. **Next:** Multiplicative sensitivity is an established computational alternative, not a novel attention discovery. |
| [Urai AE (2019)](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.46331) | Human perceptual decisions across task protocols; selected full text: XML | Choice repetition relates strongly to history-dependent accumulation bias, rather than only starting point. | Task-specific conditional RT predictions need simulation with this rig and stimulus. **Next:** Compare drift-bias and starting-point history mechanisms using conditional bias versus RT. |
| [Diaz JA (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1162/imag_a_00338) | Human choice/RT modeling constrained by EEG; abstract | Neural constraints shift the inferred source of prior-probability bias toward accumulation rate. | Independent neural constraints are unavailable in the reviewed mouse trial table. **Next:** Do not assign all history effects to starting point by default. |
| [Okazawa G (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-05797-y) | Computational analysis of psychophysical kernels; selected full text: XML | Bounds, integration mechanisms and variable delays distort recovered sensory weights. | A kernel alone cannot distinguish those sources. **Next:** Forward-simulate the exact response-conditioned pulse estimator for every candidate. |
| [Stine GM (2020)](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.55365) | Perceptual strategy discrimination and modeling; earlier review: full text | Specified nonintegration strategies can mimic conventional accumulation signatures. | Conclusions depend on task and tested candidate families. **Next:** Retain fair response-process assumptions and recovery for new comparisons. |
| [Hyafil A (2023)](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.84045) | Cross-task/species behavioral tests of integration; earlier review: full text | Designed evidence manipulations constrain integration versus nonintegration accounts. | Identification depends on input correlations and task structure. **Next:** Existing-data mechanism claims require recoverability within the actual design. |
| [Pisupati S (2021)](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.55490) | Rat multisensory decisions, reward manipulations and neural disruption; selected full text: XML | Reward-dependent lapse patterns support uncertainty-guided exploration over a generic inattention explanation. | Reward manipulations and modalities differ from this mouse motion task. **Next:** Do not identify high-coherence errors or a fitted guess fraction as attention lapses. |
| [Odoemene O (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1523/jneurosci.3478-17.2018) | Unrestrained mouse visual evidence task; abstract; earlier review | Visual accumulation in freely moving mice predates the present dataset. | Discrete visual pulses and response demands differ. **Next:** Frame novelty around constraints on variability, not the existence of mouse accumulation. |
| [Reynolds JH (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2009.01.002) | Theoretical review introducing an attention normalization model; selected full text: author PDF | Normalization can produce distinct gain effects depending on stimulus and attention-field configuration. | Not new empirical evidence and not equivalent to the already fitted scalar normalization family. **Next:** Use for mechanisms and predictions, not as proof that another normalization fit will solve the mismatch. |
| [Wong KF (2007)](https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.10.006.2007) | Recurrent circuit model of macaque motion pulses; selected full text: XML | Recurrent dynamics reproduce persistent pulse effects and timing dependence. | Computational agreement is not unique circuit identification. **Next:** Attractors remain an alternative, but a larger circuit model needs an additional discriminating signature. |
| [McGaughey KD (2026)](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.110685) | Macaque RDK, MT recordings and pupillometry; selected full text: XML | Temporal context changes sensory adaptation and behavior; MT and pupil measures provide partly separate explanatory contributions. | Observational links do not identify an accumulator algorithm; adapter/test design differs from brief pulses. **Next:** Test context-dependent encoding before interpreting behavioral weighting as memory leak. |
| [Cohen MR (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2439) | Macaque attention/population recordings; abstract and available PMC text | Attention-related performance changes include altered interneuronal correlations. | Neural population noise is not identifiable from a scalar behavioral gain parameter. **Next:** Distinguish sensory noise, gain and decision noise. |
| [Levi AJ (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1523/eneuro.0169-18.2018) | Human/macaque time-varying motion statistics; abstract and publisher methods excerpt | Temporal evidence weights adapt to the time course of stimulus information. | Experimentally manipulated statistics differ from the present fixed pulse schedule. **Next:** Treat temporal weighting as potentially strategic rather than a direct memory filter. |
| [Stine GM (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.05.028) | Macaque recordings and SC intervention; earlier review: full text | Independent neural and causal measurements distinguish accumulation from decision termination. | Behavior-only data cannot support an equivalent circuit-level claim. **Next:** Use as a benchmark for specificity and calibrate the publication claim. |
| [Murphy PR (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13526) | Human deadline task, EEG, pupillometry and modeling; selected full text: XML | Speed pressure induces urgency-related motor preparation; pupil-linked gain is a candidate implementation. | Global neural gain is not equivalent to multiplying only the signed sensory input. **Next:** Keep sensory gain, evidence-independent urgency and bound changes distinct. |
| [Wyart V (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2012.09.015) | Human multisample categorization and EEG; selected full text: PMC web | Evidence weighting fluctuates with slow oscillatory phase rather than remaining constant within trials. | The present pulse schedule lacks a direct phase measure or broad phase-sampling design. **Next:** Do not fit an oscillatory attention account from a few response-conditioned pulse bins. |
