# adelson1985spatiotemporal

**Citation:** Adelson EH, Bergen JR. *Spatiotemporal energy models for the perception of motion.* Journal of the Optical Society of America A. 1985;2:284–299. DOI: 10.1364/JOSAA.2.000284. PMID: none identified. Access: publisher abstract and figure list.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** General computational account of motion perception; no animal preparation or decision task. The model filters image sequences with oriented space-time filters, combines quadrature-pair outputs by squaring and summing, and forms opponent motion-energy signals; no brain region is tested.

**Key findings:** The publisher abstract defines the linear-filter, quadrature-energy and opponent stages used to derive direction-selective motion energy (abstract). It states that these components are compatible with known physiology and psychophysics but presents the method as a model, not a frame-veridical display measurement (abstract).

**Relevance:** Methods precedent for C003 and Figure 2. It supports the filter architecture, not the accuracy of this project's stimulus replay.

**Limitations stated/visible:** The abstract is qualitative about biological compatibility; the full paper was not assessed here for parameter choices matching this implementation.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Spatiotemporal motion energy can be computed by oriented linear filters followed by quadrature squaring/summing and opponent subtraction (publisher abstract).
