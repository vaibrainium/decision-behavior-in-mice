# luken2025parameter

**Citation:** Lüken M, Heathcote A, Haaf JM, Matzke D. *Parameter identifiability in evidence-accumulation models: The effect of error rates on the diffusion decision model and the linear ballistic accumulator.* Psychonomic Bulletin & Review. 2025;32:1411–1424. DOI: 10.3758/s13423-024-02621-1. PMID: 39777607. Access: PubMed abstract and publisher PDF summary.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Simulation study assessed parameter identifiability and estimation in diffusion and linear-ballistic evidence-accumulation models across trial counts and error rates.

**Key findings:** Both models had poor parameter identifiability at low error rates, especially with fewer trials; trade-offs linked decision threshold and accumulation rate (PubMed abstract).

**Relevance:** Recent counter-evidence against interpreting fitted parameters mechanistically without recovery, especially for high-coherence conditions.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Simulation of standard DDM/LBA settings, not the present mixture model or pulse-specific gain.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Evidence-accumulation parameters can be poorly identified when errors are rare or trial counts are limited, so parameter-level mechanisms require recovery checks (abstract).
