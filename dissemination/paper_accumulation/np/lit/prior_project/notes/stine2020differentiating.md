# stine2020differentiating

Stine GM, Zylberberg A, Ditterich J, Shadlen MN (2020). *Differentiating between integration and non-integration strategies in perceptual decision making*. eLife. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.55365; PMID: 32338595. Access: full publisher article and PMC7217695.

- **Question / method:** Which behavioural measurements distinguish integration from extrema/non-integration strategies?
- **Key findings:** Non-integration strategies can mimic accuracy and apparently flat temporal kernels; joint choice and response-time constraints improve discrimination. Locator: Abstract; Results sections "Non-integration strategies..." and free-response model comparisons.
- **Relevance:** Central closest prior and motivation for the control-model comparison. Also warns that pulse sensitivity alone does not prove accumulation.
- **Limitations for our use:** Establishes identification principles, not that one model fitted on controls will generalize to strong transient evidence.
- **Paraphrase for C001/C004:** Choice accuracy and kernels can be model-degenerate, whereas joint choice/RT prediction is more constraining.
