# bogacz2006physics

**Citation:** Bogacz R, Brown E, Moehlis J, Holmes P, Cohen JD. 2006. The physics of optimal decision making: a formal analysis of models of performance in two-alternative forced-choice tasks. *Psychological Review* 113:700–765. DOI: 10.1037/0033-295X.113.4.700. PMID: 17014301.

**Access:** abstract only, PubMed. **Question:** How do prominent two-alternative decision models relate to the statistically optimal drift-diffusion algorithm? **Method:** formal analysis of six models and speed–accuracy objectives.

**Key findings:** The abstract states that all but one analyzed model reduce to drift diffusion under relevant assumptions and derives optimal speed–accuracy trade-offs under several objectives.

**Relevance:** theoretical basis for the accumulator family and for comparing complete generative predictions. **Limitation:** formal equivalence under assumptions does not establish which implementation animals use.
