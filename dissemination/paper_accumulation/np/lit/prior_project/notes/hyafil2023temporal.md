# hyafil2023temporal

**Citation:** Hyafil A, de la Rocha J, Pericas C, Katz LN, Huk AC, Pillow JW. 2023. Temporal integration is a robust feature of perceptual decisions. *eLife* 12:e84045. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.84045. PMID: 37140191.

**Access:** full text, eLife. **Question:** Can discrete-sample behavioural data distinguish temporal integration from extrema-detection and snapshot strategies? **Species/tasks:** macaques, humans and rats; multiple sensory discrimination datasets with experimenter-controlled samples. **Method:** fitted integration/extrema/snapshot models, psychophysical kernels, accuracy bounds, disagree trials and early–late integration maps.

**Key findings:** Across included datasets, integration models fit better than tested non-integration models and weak evidence plus both early and late evidence contributed to choices (Abstract; Results, Figures 2–6). Some intuitive subjective-weight analyses were inconclusive after fitted sensory noise was considered (Results, subjective weighting). The authors state that their variants do not exhaust all possible non-integration policies (Discussion).

**Relevance:** closest prior support for integration over extrema detection; forces the present paper to emphasize joint reaction-time/censoring prediction and out-of-condition pulse generalization. **Limitations:** analyses depend on discrete evidence samples controlled and known precisely, unlike an approximate display replay.
