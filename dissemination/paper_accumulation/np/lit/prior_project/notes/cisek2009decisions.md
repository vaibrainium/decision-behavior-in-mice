# cisek2009decisions

**Citation:** Cisek P, Puskas GA, El-Murr S. *Decisions in changing conditions: the urgency-gating model.* Journal of Neuroscience. 2009;29:11560–11571. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1844-09.2009. PMID: 19759303. Access: PubMed abstract; PMC full text available.

**Question / preparation / task / method / regions:** Humans judged time-varying motion evidence; behavioural predictions of temporal integration were contrasted with momentary sensory estimates multiplied by a growing urgency signal.

**Key findings:** Behaviour in the changing-evidence task was more consistent with the tested urgency-gating account than with temporal integration (PubMed abstract, final sentences).

**Relevance:** Supports H2 as a substantive alternative and explains why constant-evidence tasks may not identify integration.

**Limitations stated/visible:** Human task and one model contrast; urgency-gating success does not establish the same mechanism for mouse pulses or all tasks.

**Claim-ready paraphrase:** Time-varying evidence can favor an urgency-gating account over perfect temporal integration even when constant-evidence behaviour is compatible with either model (abstract).
