Panel D. Group-level recovery from simulated behavior. Rows identify the model used to generate choices and RTs. Each percentage is the fraction of1,024 recombined ten-mouse cohorts for which the original group analysis favored C over the indicated alternative. The C-versus-E and C-versus-ES rates are identical in every displayed row, so a single percentage is shown for both comparisons: C-generated100%, E-generated0%, ES-generated0%. “Favored C” requires a positive mean normalized held-out NLL advantage and a two-sided mouse-level t-test with Holm-adjusted p<0.05 across the four alternative comparisons. This is not five-way winner selection. Cohorts select one of two simulated dataset replicates per mouse (2^10 combinations); they reuse datasets and are not independent experiments. Percentages are conditional on the tested generating parameters, schedules and candidate set; they are not universal false-positive rates. Values are unchanged from the preceding D.
