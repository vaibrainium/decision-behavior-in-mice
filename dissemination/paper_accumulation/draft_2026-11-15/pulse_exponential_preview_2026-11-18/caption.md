Standalone exploratory exponential description of the existing dilution-adjusted pulse readout. Black points and intervals are the unchanged estimates and percentile mouse-bootstrap 95% intervals for the six bins shown in Figure 4D. Horizontal positions are the actual mean pulse-to-response latencies; unequal bin widths are not represented as ordinal steps. The blue curve fits y=A exp[−(t−t_ref)/τ], with a fixed zero asymptote and nonnegative amplitude, by unweighted least squares across bins. All six bins and the negative late estimate are retained. Blue shading propagates the saved whole-mouse bootstrap readouts through the fit, conditional on the observed time coordinates. This curve is a descriptive approximation to a plateau followed by a decline, not a fitted decision process or a direct intrinsic forgetting-time estimate. Figure 4 remains unchanged.
