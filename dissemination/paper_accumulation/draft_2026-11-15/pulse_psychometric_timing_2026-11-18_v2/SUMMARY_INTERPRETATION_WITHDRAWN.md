# Timing coefficient average: interpretation withdrawn

The previous early/middle mean-of-individual-coefficients preview must not be interpreted as the population psychometric midpoint or evidence that early left/right pulses have equal effects. Individual time-split curves are weakly identified and one early-left fit reaches alpha=-100. Averaging these estimates creates a misleading population summary. Free asymptotes also change the fitted response range whose midpoint alpha denotes; alpha is not necessarily the P(right)=.5 criterion.
