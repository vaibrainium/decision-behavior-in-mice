**D, Control accuracy conditional on response time.** Scheduled control trials from the model-analysis population (14,014 trials; 10 mice). Trials were grouped by absolute motion coherence and response time on a 400-ms grid. Within each mouse, accuracy was calculated separately in each occupied coherence × time cell; points and connecting lines show the equal-mouse mean, with shading indicating ±SEM across contributing mice. Each group cell contains 5–10 mice; all occupied cells were retained, including sparse individual cells. The first and last intervals are clipped to the analysis response window (300–400 and 4,800–5,000 ms). At zero coherence, correctness denotes agreement with the task-assigned target side. The horizontal dotted line indicates 0.5. This response-conditioned summary is descriptive and does not independently identify a decision bound.
