Figure 6a variation. Best all-trial model fit preview. The power-law input model with leak, urgency, and persistent across-trial drift variability was selected by both equal-mouse held-out joint bits per trial and full-fit BIC in the all-trial comparison. Columns show control trials and early, middle, and late pulse conditions. Points are equal-mouse observed means; vertical error bars and RT ribbons are between-mouse SEM. Downward blue and upward orange triangles denote leftward and rightward pulses. (A) Right-choice probability. Observed probabilities are points with between-mouse SEM; open diamonds are saved model probabilities at the same tested coherences, joined by straight segments. No psychometric function is fitted to either series for display. (B) Arithmetic mean response time across both choices. Dotted point-lines show observations and solid lines show model predictions at tested coherences. The decision model was fitted jointly to control and pulse trials. Displayed predictions use saved full-fit parameters and simulations of each mouse's responded trial schedules (30 repetitions, seed 0); therefore the overlays are descriptive fits, not held-out validation. Model summaries are matched to the exact observed mouse/condition/coherence support. The display population is the fit browser's symmetric scheduled-pulse responded-trial selection and is not the narrower post-pulse-end selection used in Figure 4.
