# Proposed Figure 3A. Cohort summary of direct held-out NLL

**(A)** Bars show the unweighted mean across 10 mice of each model's total held-out negative log likelihood, summed over five cross-validation folds per mouse. Error bars show SEM across mice (sample standard deviation divided by sqrt(10)). Lower NLL indicates better prediction under the saved scoring procedure. Each mouse's candidate models use the same held-out control trials. All bars start at zero. No reference subtraction or per-trial normalization is applied. Subject trial counts differ; consequently this descriptive summary of raw totals reflects trial counts and response distributions as well as model fit. The SEM represents between-mouse variation in total scores and does not estimate uncertainty in the paired model differences, fitted parameters or Monte Carlo simulation. No significance or equivalence claim is based on overlap of these intervals.

Sources: absolute_nll.csv, fold_scores.csv and group_nll.csv. Underlying raw scores and fold counts are unchanged from the preceding per-mouse preview. Only panel A is revised. Per-mouse display remains available with --display mouse. Reproduce with scripts.paper.figure3_raw_nll --output <fresh-directory> (default --display group).
