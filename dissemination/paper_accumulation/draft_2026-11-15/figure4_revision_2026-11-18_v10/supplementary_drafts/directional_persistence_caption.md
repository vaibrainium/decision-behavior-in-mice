Directional pulse influence as a function of response latency from pulse onset. The pooled logistic decomposition includes categorical coherence-by-latency-bin baselines, categorical mouse intercepts, a directional pulse term and a shared term toward the baseline-correct side. Only the directional coefficient is displayed here; the full decomposition is retained in the supplement draft. Points show coefficients and vertical lines show percentile 95% intervals from 300 mouse-bootstrap refits. Asterisks mark Holm-adjusted bootstrap p<0.05 across seven directional bins; the three bins spanning 0.3–1.2 s each have adjusted p=0.0233. The early/middle-onset analysis includes 19,313 eligible responses from 10 mice (8,529 pulse responses), with symmetric pulse schedules, null pulses excluded, responses after actual or virtual pulse offset, and at least 0.1 s after onset. Controls use matched virtual onsets. Zero-coherence responses contribute to the directional term but not the signed shared term. Late-onset pulse and matched-control strata are excluded here, unlike the all-available onset-stage model in C. The curve shows sustained directional influence through responses made 0.9–1.2 s after pulse onset; it does not estimate an intrinsic integration time constant.
