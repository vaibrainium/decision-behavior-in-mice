# Figure 3 captions — current working order

## A — Conditional accuracy

**A, Control accuracy conditional on response time, with descriptive saturation fits.** Points show equal-mouse mean accuracy in the existing response-time bins; shading is ±SEM across contributing mice. Solid curves are monotonic exponential fits for nonzero coherence, fitted to individual correct/error outcomes with each mouse contributing equal total weight within coherence. The 0% curve is a fitted constant. The exponential starts at the 300-ms analysis limit with freely estimated starting accuracy and asymptote. Because mice have different RT distributions, equal-mouse trial-weighted fits need not pass through the equal-mouse binned means. Curves are descriptive and their time constants are not estimates of a mechanistic integration time. Data selection and binning match the previous Figure3D candidate. Whole-mouse bootstrap intervals are shown separately in the diagnostic figure; the main shading remains data SEM.

## B — Model comparison, alternatives retained

The source captions below retain historical A1/A2 names; these alternatives now occupy panel B.

Two alternatives for panel A, both with two y-axes. Blue solid circles: left test LL in A1, left joint choice/RT test log score in bits/trial in A2; higher is better. Orange dashed squares: right mean training QMLE-based BIC, lower is better. Equal-mouse means across 10 mice; BIC is averaged over existing independent scoring seeds within mouse. LL = −NLL. Bits/trial = LL/(n_test ln2) within mouse before averaging; not choice-only gain over a null. Axes use independently labeled nonzero ranges. Curve intersections have no statistical meaning. No error bars, tests, new fits or scoring.

## C — Selected model fits

Panel C. Model C fits to control psychometric and chronometric functions. Left: probability of a right choice versus signed coherence. Right: arithmetic mean RT conditional on right choice (orange circles) or left choice (blue squares). Points are equal-mouse observed means; shaded bands are pointwise SEM across contributing mice. Psychometric panel: dashed gray and solid black curves are separate descriptive four-parameter logistic fits to the equal-mouse observed and model-predicted proportions, respectively. The exact author equation is p(x) = lapse_rate + (1 − guess_rate − lapse_rate)/(1 + exp[−var(x − mean)]); fitting uses the same helper/default bounds as Figure 1 and unweighted least squares on group proportions. Model C logistic smoothing is not an analytical DDM prediction or a new DDM fit. Chronometric solid lines show predictions at tested coherences from each mouse’s saved full-control model C parameters, pooled across 20 simulations per input within mouse before averaging mice. Curves describe fits to the control data used for parameter estimation; unseen-control prediction is assessed separately by panel B’s held-out scores. RT model/data curves use identical mouse/coherence/choice cells with at least 10 observed trials; 7–10 mice contribute per RT cell. The population contains 14,014 modeled controls. Figure 1 contains one additional PSUIM7 trial with missing pulse-duration metadata absent from model inputs. No new DDM fitting, simulation, scoring or significance tests. No pulse trials analyzed.
