Audit of timing-specific psychometric summaries. Choice-curve points are unweighted means of within-mouse right-choice proportions, with across-mouse SEM, for the same10 mice at early and middle pulse times. Lines are a jointly fitted descriptive population model in which control/left/right curves share estimated asymptotes within each time but have separate free midpoints and positive slopes. No direction or time trend is imposed. Population-parameter panel shows absolute midpoint α and slope change relative to control, with120-resample whole-mouse bootstrap95% percentile intervals. This estimates parameters of population curves rather than means of individual coefficients. Original individual means are withdrawn as a population interpretation; existing Figure4 unchanged.
