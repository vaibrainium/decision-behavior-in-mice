# Supplementary draft: former Figure 4C

Logistic decomposition of pulse influence in seven response-latency bins. The directional coefficient D shifts choices toward the physical pulse direction. The shared coefficient S describes a shift toward the baseline-correct side for either pulse direction; zero-coherence trials contribute no signed shared term. Fits include mouse intercepts and coherence-by-bin baselines. Points are coefficients; vertical lines are percentile 95% intervals from 300 mouse-bootstrap refits. Asterisks indicate Holm-adjusted bootstrap p < 0.05 across seven bins within each coefficient family. Directional effects persist through the 0.9–1.2-s bin; no shared-effect bin passes that correction.
