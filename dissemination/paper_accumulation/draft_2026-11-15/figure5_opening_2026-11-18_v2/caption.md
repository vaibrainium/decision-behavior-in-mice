(A) Components varied across the existing pulse-only accumulation fits: input mapping (linear, power law or normalization), accumulator dynamics (integration, leak, urgency, leak plus urgency, or a restricted urgency-gating region), and presence or absence of persistent across-trial drift variability. This diagram is a conceptual summary, not a fully crossed design or simulated trajectory. All candidates were fitted to pulse trials; control-only and joint-fit candidates are excluded. (B) Existing held-out NLL and independently rescored full-fit BIC for all 22 pulse-only candidates, ranked by held-out NLL in both columns. Scores are summed over 10 individually fitted mice; the common modeled population contains 11,479 pulse trials. Held-out scores sum five test folds per mouse; BIC is a distinct full-fit criterion using the original free-parameter counts and independent training rescoring. Blue marks the lowest held-out score; orange marks the lowest BIC. Other candidates are gray. Points are descriptive cohort totals, without inferential error bars or a new group significance test. The predictive winner is normalized input in the urgency-gating region without drift variability; BIC instead favors the linear-input leaky accumulator with drift variability. Unequal candidate coverage limits attribution of the difference to any one component. No new fitting or scoring was performed.
