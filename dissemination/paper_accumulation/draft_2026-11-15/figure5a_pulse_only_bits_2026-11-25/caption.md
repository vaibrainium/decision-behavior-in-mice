Figure 5 bits/trial ranking preview. (A) Components varied across the existing pulse-only accumulation fits. The diagram is a conceptual summary, not a fully crossed design or simulated trajectory. (B) The 22 pulse-only candidates are ordered by equal-mouse mean held-out joint log score in bits per trial (higher, or less negative, is better). For each mouse, bits/trial equals -held-out NLL divided by the number of held-out trials and ln(2); the plotted value then averages the 10 mice equally. This is a joint choice-and-response-time score, not choice-only information gain over a null model. The adjacent BIC values use the same candidate order but are independently rescored full-fit criteria; lower is better. Blue marks the best bits/trial candidate and orange the lowest-BIC candidate. The predictive winner remains normalized input in the urgency-gating region without drift variability; BIC favors the linear-input leaky accumulator with drift variability. The lower-rank order can differ from summed NLL because equal-mouse bits/trial and cohort-total NLL weight mice differently. Points are descriptive, with no inferential error bars or new group test. No fitting or rescoring was performed.
