# Supplementary draft: assignment-based pulse influence

Assignment-based directional response contrasts, a different estimand from the response-conditioned curves in A, C and D. Responses by 5 s were coded +0.5 for right, −0.5 for left and 0 for no response. Right-pulse minus left-pulse mean scores were computed within mouse × session × signed-coherence × onset cells, weighted within mouse by the smaller assignment count in each pair, and multiplied by 100. Squares denote UW mice and circles UCLA mice; apparatus, cohort and schedule are confounded. The black diamond and horizontal interval show the unweighted mouse mean and its 95% t interval: 5.84 percentage points [1.80, 9.89]. The previously reported Holm-adjusted p is 0.0194, corrected across the three primary directional contrasts (right versus left pulse, right pulse versus control, and control versus left pulse). Source tables: `source_data/fig4_*`; correction provenance: `dissemination/reviewer_2026-11-13/COMPLETED_RESULTS.md` and its referenced behavioral group table.
