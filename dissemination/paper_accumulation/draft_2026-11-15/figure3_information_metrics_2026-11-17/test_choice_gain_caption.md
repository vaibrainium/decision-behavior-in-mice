# GLM-HMM-style held-out choice gain in bits per trial

Points show the equal-mouse mean choice log-score gain over a training-set choice-bias baseline: (LL_choice,test − LL_null,test)/[n_test ln(2)]. The archived choice-only test score is pooled across five folds per mouse. For each fold, the null probability of a right choice is the fraction of right choices among that mouse's training control trials only; the same fixed probability scores each held-out choice. The null contains no stimulus predictor. Higher values indicate better choice prediction relative to that baseline; zero is no gain. Ten mice contribute. No RT term is included. No error bars or new significance tests are shown; the line joins categorical values as a guide.

Definition follows Ashwood et al., Mice alternate between discrete strategies during perceptual decision-making, Eq. 22 (2021 preprint / 2022 publication): https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-01007-z. The paper's baseline is learned on training choices, not the held-out set; the same rule is used here. This is a choice-only companion to the joint choice+RT score, not an interchangeable likelihood. Archived choice components and fold-specific training nulls are exported in test_bits_per_mouse.csv and training_only_choice_nulls.csv. The earlier fixed-RT-bin bits outputs were not reused.
