# QMLE-based ΔBIC for full-control fits

Points show the equal-mouse mean ΔBIC = BIC_model − BIC_C. Positive values favor C, negative values favor the alternative, and C is zero by definition. For each mouse and model, BIC = 2 NLL_training + k ln(n_training), with the full-control parameters held fixed and the original fitting objective independently evaluated at seeds 12345 and 23456, using 30 simulation repetitions per input. BIC differences are calculated within mouse and seed, averaged over seeds, then averaged equally over 10 mice. Five-fold held-out scores are not used to calculate BIC. Actual saved free-parameter counts are C 9, E 10, ES 8, DDMx 4 and ESx 4; training populations match the control comparison populations for every mouse. Lines join categorical model means; no inferential error bars or significance stars are added.

The formula and reference-difference convention follow Stine et al. (2020), Eq. 17 and comparison tables: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.55365. Here BIC is a QMLE-based approximation, evaluated at saved parameter estimates, not an exact marginal likelihood or Bayes factor for continuous RTs. Independent scoring addresses optimization over the original simulation draw but does not eliminate simulation/parameter uncertainty. Individual-seed scores and group differences are retained for inspection. Full-control fits use the same candidate rules as CV; C's all-control parameters are saved in fits_resp_final, while its CV fits are in fits_resp_fast_only. This distinction is explicit in the fit inventory. Earlier independently rescored IC tables covered different model sets and were not substituted for these five candidates.
