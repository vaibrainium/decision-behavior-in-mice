Psychometric panel: dashed gray and solid black curves are separate descriptive four-parameter logistic fits to the equal-mouse observed and model-predicted proportions, respectively. The exact author equation is p(x) = lapse_rate + (1 − guess_rate − lapse_rate)/(1 + exp[−var(x − mean)]); fitting uses the same helper/default bounds as Figure 1 and unweighted least squares on group proportions. Model C logistic smoothing is not an analytical DDM prediction or a new DDM fit. Chronometric solid lines show predictions at tested coherences from each mouse’s saved full-control model C parameters, pooled across 20 simulations per input within mouse before averaging mice. Curves describe fits to the control data used for parameter estimation; unseen-control prediction is assessed separately by panel A’s held-out scores. RT model/data curves use identical mouse/coherence/choice cells with at least 10 observed trials; 7–10 mice contribute per RT cell. The population contains 14,014 modeled controls.
