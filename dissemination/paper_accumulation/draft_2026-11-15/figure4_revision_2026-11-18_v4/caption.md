## Figure 4. Brief motion pulses exert a sustained directional influence on choices

**(A)** Psychometric functions for left-pulse (blue downward triangles) and right-pulse (orange upward triangles) trials. Control trials are omitted from this panel. Points show unweighted means of per-mouse right-choice proportions; error bars show SEM across available mice. Solid curves are separate descriptive four-parameter logistic fits using the author’s logit_4 equation and default bounds, fitted without trial-count weighting across coherence levels to these equal-mouse means. Both fitted curves are displayed from −100% to +100% coherence; portions beyond the observed range of a pulse direction are descriptive extrapolations, with no additional data points. The plotted pulse conditions contain 8,529 eligible responses from 10 mice under the existing symmetric-schedule, early/middle-onset selection, with responses after the pulse ended and pulse-to-response interval at least 0.1 s. These curves are descriptive psychometric fits, not new decision-model fits or a test of their separation.

**(B)** Paired psychometric parameters for physical left- and right-pulse conditions. Each line connects one mouse (10 mice; all retained). Separate individual four-parameter logit_4 curves were fitted using trial-count-weighted least squares, as in individual Figure1 fits. α is the logistic horizontal midpoint (`mean`); β is its coefficient (`var`), shown on a logarithmic axis. With asymmetric lower/upper asymptotes, α need not equal the coherence giving P(right)=0.5. The tests use raw coefficient differences, right minus left, and not the plotted log β values. Two-sided exact sign-flip tests of the mean paired differences enumerate all1,024 sign patterns; p values are Holm-adjusted across α and β. Mean α difference was−18.36 percentage-coherence units (median−16.38; signed dz−0.81; unadjusted t-based95% CI−34.64 to−2.07); Holm-adjusted exact p=0.0703. Mean β difference was−0.3023 per percentage-coherence unit (median−0.00860; signed dz−0.22; unadjusted t-based95% CI−1.2779 to0.6733); Holm-adjusted exact p=0.7031. Several fits are weakly constrained or boundary-limited; this exploratory comparison conditions on fitted coefficients and is not definitive evidence of a mechanistic slope/bias change. Shared-control-asymptote sensitivity estimates and fit-quality diagnostics are exported and do not replace the primary unrestricted fits.

**(C)** Logistic decomposition of pulse influence in seven response-latency bins. The directional coefficient D shifts choices toward the physical pulse direction. The shared coefficient S describes a shift toward the baseline-correct side for either pulse direction; zero-coherence trials contribute no signed shared term. Fits include mouse intercepts and coherence-by-bin baselines. Points are coefficients; vertical lines are percentile 95% intervals from 300 mouse-bootstrap refits. Asterisks indicate Holm-adjusted bootstrap p < 0.05 across seven bins within each coefficient family. Directional effects persist through the 0.9–1.2-s bin; no shared-effect bin passes that correction.

**(D)** Coherence-equivalent pulse influence multiplied by mean response time from motion onset, γT. The coherence-equivalent influence γ is the separately estimated directional log-odds coefficient divided by the coherence slope. Points show the observed readout; vertical lines show percentile 95% intervals from 600 mouse-bootstrap samples. The dotted line marks the unweighted mean over the 0.3–1.2-s bins. This additional dilution-adjusted analysis retains the observed later decline. Its behavioral time course constrains model predictions without directly estimating an intrinsic integration time constant. The x axes in C–D use discrete bins; spacing does not represent equal physical durations.

**(E)** Assignment-based directional response contrasts, a different estimand from the response-conditioned curves in A, C and D. Responses by 5 s were coded +0.5 for right, −0.5 for left and 0 for no response. Right-pulse minus left-pulse mean scores were computed within mouse × session × signed-coherence × onset cells, weighted within mouse by the smaller assignment count in each pair, and multiplied by 100. Squares denote UW mice and circles UCLA mice; apparatus, cohort and schedule are confounded. The black diamond and horizontal interval show the unweighted mouse mean and its 95% t interval: 5.84 percentage points [1.80, 9.89]. The previously reported Holm-adjusted p is 0.0194, corrected across the three primary directional contrasts (right versus left pulse, right pulse versus control, and control versus left pulse). Source tables: `source_data/fig4_*`; correction provenance: `dissemination/reviewer_2026-11-13/COMPLETED_RESULTS.md` and its referenced behavioral group table.
