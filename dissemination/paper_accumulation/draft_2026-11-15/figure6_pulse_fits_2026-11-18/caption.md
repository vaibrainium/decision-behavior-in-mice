Controls, including control observations previously borrowed by the browser to anchor psychometric endpoints, are omitted. Population is the browser's responded-trial symmetric scheduled-pulse selection, including all onset stages; it is not the narrower post-pulse-end selection of Figure 4. Simulations used each mouse's responded input schedules, 30 repetitions and seed 0, as documented in build_fit_browser.py and pulse_joint_signatures.py.
