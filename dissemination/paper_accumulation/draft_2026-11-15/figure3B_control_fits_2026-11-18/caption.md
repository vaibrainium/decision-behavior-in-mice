Panel B. Control psychometric and chronometric functions with model fits. Columns show C, E, ES, DDMx and ESx. Top: P(right choice) versus signed coherence. Bottom: mean RT conditional on right (orange circles) or left choice (blue squares), using solid model traces. Points show observed equal-mouse means and shaded bands show pointwise SEM across contributing mice. Lines are fixed-parameter predictions from saved full-control fits on each mouse's actual control input schedules, conditional on a simulated response between 0.3 and 5 s. Predictions pool response counts/RT sums across 20 repetitions within mouse before averaging mice. These are descriptive in-sample fits, distinct from A's held-out test score. RT curves use the same mouse/coherence/choice cells as data, restricted to at least 10 observed trials per cell. Mouse counts vary by cell and are exported. All 14,014 modeled controls are included.
