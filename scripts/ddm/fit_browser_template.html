Pulse-task fit browser

Data and model by trial type: control trials, and pulses early, middle or late in the trial toward the left or right. Each model is simulated on every mouse's own responded trials (30 repetitions). All coherences including ±100% (pulse trials exist there too); trials from the symmetric pulse design, as in the pulse analyses. Positive coherence = rightward motion; a negative psychometric mean = rightward bias.
