## Question
1. Does the held-out ordering of the top 4 models hold across Monte Carlo seeds and across mice?
2. In the data alone, does the pulse effect shrink with coherence or depend on timing?
3. Is the +10 pp early, zero-coherence model residual robust, or mostly PSUIM6/7?

## Summary
Norm UGM without sv has the lowest held-out NLL against each of the other three at all 5 seeds (by 52 to 62 NLL). Most of that lead comes from PSUIM6/7; in the other 8 mice the differences are within ±9 NLL. The early-pulse residual is also mostly PSUIM6/7. Without them, the early zero-coherence residual is 2 to 4 pp and its CI includes 0. In the data alone the pulse effect does not clearly shrink with coherence. Early pulses have 3.6 to 6.8 pp effects at every coherence, with wide CIs. The "effect shrinks with coherence" idea is not supported.
