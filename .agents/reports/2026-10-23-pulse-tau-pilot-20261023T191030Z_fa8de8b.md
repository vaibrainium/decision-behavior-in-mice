## Question
Would one bounded extension of the existing model reconcile the pulse-trial misfit found in package E? The extension
passes the pulse component through a causal exponential with time constant tau_p. The misfit has two parts: the
choice-only and joint pulse gains disagree, and the model reverses the directional effect late.

## Summary
**FAIL under the frozen rule. Modelling stops.** A slower pulse response (tau_p = 300 ms, the grid edge) improves the
joint fit in both mice, in sample and in 4 of 5 held-out folds each. It does **not** bring the choice and RT gains
together, and it does **not** remove the late reversal. Criteria P2 and P3 fail in both mice. The frozen P0 check
flagged float32 rounding; that does not affect the decision (see the P0 finding). Confidence that the candidate does
not fix the mismatch in these two mice: moderate to high. Generality: low, because only 2 mice from one rig were tested.

- VERIFIED (P0 reassessed). The check required the control pulse component to be exactly zero. Its measured maximum is
  1.19e-07, which is float32 rounding of S − c × onset response (already documented in `primacy.check_inputs`) and
  2.2e-07 of the pulse amplitude. The pilot simulates only pulse trials, so control predictions cannot change. The
  check was numerically over-strict; this is not an implementation error. It does not affect the outcome, because P2 and
  P3 fail regardless. Recorded in `tau_pilot/decision_addendum.json`.
- VERIFIED. tau_p = 0 on v2 and package E on v1 give total NLL higher by 16–29 on v2. That is expected: v2 adds 17
  (PSUIM6) and 12 (PSUIM7) relabelled pulse trials, so the two totals are not comparable. The choice terms differ by ≤ 1.2 NLL.
  On v2 the joint gain at tau_p = 0 is 3.0 for PSUIM6 (E on v1: 2.45) and 1.5 for PSUIM7 (E: 1.40).
- SUSPECTED. The fit improvement at the grid edge (300 ms) says longer pulse persistence buys some likelihood. Per
  the spec the grid was not extended. Even so, the improvement does not come from fixing the time course: PSUIM7's late
  reversal got worse at the best point. The gain may be trading against tau_p.

## Caveats
- Only 2 mice, from one rig, carry enough data. That rig's display timing is unresolved and its pulse energy may be
  understated ×1.28–1.40 (package B); this trades with w, not with tau_p.
- The in-sample grid and the fold held-out scores use the same trials; the analysis is retrospective and exploratory.
- The grid is coarse (3 tau × 8 w), and its best point sits on the edge.
- The control parameters stay frozen, so a joint refit could behave differently. That would be a new model, outside this plan.
- A FAIL says this candidate does not reconcile the mismatch. It does not show that accumulation is absent, that there
  is no primacy, or that no other model would succeed.
